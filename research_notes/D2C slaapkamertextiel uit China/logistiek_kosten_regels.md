# Logistics, import costs and EU/NL regulations: importing bed linen and bedding from China for a D2C webshop (NL/BE, later DE)

Research date: 4 October 2026. All prices are dated. Labels used below:
- **[FACT]**: confirmed by a primary or official source.
- **[INDEX]**: a market benchmark, which moves every week.
- **[AGGREGATOR]**: a secondary or marketing source (forwarder blogs and similar). Treat as an estimate.
- **[BACKGROUND, UNVERIFIED]**: from general domain knowledge. It was not fetched or confirmed in this session and must be verified before use.

Two sourcing models are compared throughout:
- **Model A**: air freight per order or batch from factory stock.
- **Model B**: sea container (or LCL) into local NL stock with a 3PL.

## 1. Air freight China to NL (AMS): general cargo, express, e-commerce lines, volumetric weight, transit

### Takeaway
In late September 2026, the China/Far East to Europe air cargo benchmark (airport-to-airport, general cargo) is about **USD 4.1–4.9/kg**, down from a 2026 peak of about USD 5–6.6/kg in spring. That spring peak followed fuel-cost rises and a war-related disruption. I found no reliable published per-kg rates for express couriers or e-commerce lines (Yanwen, 4PX, Cainiao) for 1–5 kg parcels. Only transit times were found (7–25 working days). Bulky duvets and pillows will be billed on volumetric weight unless they are vacuum-packed.

- Context [INDEX]: Drewry's 1 Oct 2026 commentary mentions ongoing "Strait of Hormuz disruptions" and Houthi risk. Fuel and geopolitical risk therefore still affect air rates. — [Drewry WCI 1 Oct 2026](https://www.drewry.co.uk/supply-chain-advisors/supply-chain-expertise/world-container-index-assessed-by-drewry)

### Inferences
- **Index vs. what you actually pay.** The index is an airport-to-airport, linehaul-only price for general cargo. Door-to-door consolidated air (50–500 kg) also includes:
  - Chinese pickup and export clearance
  - fuel and security surcharges
  - AMS handling
  - NL customs brokerage
  - last-mile delivery

  A realistic estimate is roughly **index + EUR 1–3/kg plus fixed per-shipment fees** (estimate only; get quotes).
- **Volumetric weight** [BACKGROUND, UNVERIFIED]:
  - Air cargo forwarders usually use 1:6000 (6,000 cm³ = 1 kg, about 167 kg/m³).
  - Express couriers (DHL/UPS/FedEx) usually use 1:5000 (about 200 kg/m³).

  Worked example: an un-compressed duvet in a 60×45×20 cm box has a volumetric weight of about 9–11 kg but an actual weight of about 1.5–2 kg. That gives roughly 5× over-charging. Vacuum or roll-packing that cuts volume by 60–75% is therefore decisive for the air model.
- **Transit times** [BACKGROUND, UNVERIFIED]:
  - Air cargo airport-to-airport: about 3–7 days.
  - Door-to-door consolidated: about 7–12 days.
  - Express (DHL/UPS/FedEx): about 3–6 days.
  - E-commerce lines: 7–25 working days (matches the citation above).
- For Model A (per-consumer-order parcels from China), the July 2026 €3 duty and the November 2026 handling fee are added per parcel (see section 2). This makes small single-item parcels much less competitive than before.

### Gaps
- No verified 2026 per-kg tariffs for DHL/UPS/FedEx Express from China to NL for 1–5 kg. Their rate cards are account-specific. Request quotes, or check the DHL Express rate guide for China export.
- No verified per-kg rates for Yanwen, 4PX or Cainiao lines for 1–5 kg textiles to NL. These rates are quote-based and change often.
- No verified door-to-door consolidated air quote for 50–500 kg. Obtain 2–3 forwarder quotes, for example via the Freightos marketplace.

## 2. Shipping D2C from China to EU consumers: IOSS, end of the €150 duty exemption, €3 duty, handling fee

### Takeaway
Since **1 July 2026**, a **fixed €3 customs duty** applies to low-value (<€150) e-commerce consignments from IOSS-registered sellers. It applies per parcel; see the conflict note in Inferences.

A separate **customs handling fee** has also been legislated. The amount is set by Commission delegated act, and member states must apply it **no later than 1 November 2026**. The European Parliament gave final approval to the customs reform on **16 September 2026**.

Under the reform, online sellers and platforms selling from outside the EU are treated as the importer. The direct-from-China D2C model is becoming structurally more expensive and more regulated than bulk import.

### Cited Findings
- [FACT] On 12 Dec 2025, the Council agreed that customs duty will be levied on small parcels from **1 July 2026**. — [Council press release 12 Dec 2025](https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/press/press-releases/2025/12/12/customs-council-agrees-to-levy-customs-duty-on-small-parcels-as-of-1-july-2026/)
- [FACT/secondary] From 1 July 2026, goods in small consignments valued under €150 pay a **fixed €3 customs duty**. It applies to goods from non-EU sellers registered in the EU **IOSS** for VAT, which covers about 93% of e-commerce flows. It is described as temporary. — [Euronews 12 Dec 2025](https://www.euronews.com/my-europe/2025/12/12/eu-countries-agree-temporary-3-flat-customs-fee-for-small-imported-parcels); [Value Added Resource](https://www.valueaddedresource.net/eu-flat-customs-duty-fee-low-value-imports/)
- [Secondary] The €3 duty "applies per parcel, not per item": three items in one parcel pay €3, while three separate parcels pay €3 each. — [Baker Tilly SEE](https://bakertillysee.com/insights/major-eu-customs-update-e3-duty-on-low-value-e-commerce-imports-starting-july-2026/) (via search summary). See the conflict note in Inferences.
- [FACT] On 26 Mar 2026, the Council and Parliament agreed on the customs reform. It includes a **new handling fee** on small distance-sales consignments. The fee level is set by **Commission delegated act**, revised every two years, and applied by member states **no later than 1 November 2026**. — [Council press release 26 Mar 2026](https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/press/press-releases/2026/03/26/eu-customs-council-and-parliament-agree-on-landmark-reform/)
- [FACT/secondary] On 16 Sep 2026, Parliament gave final approval to the reform. Details:
  - Handling fee mandatory by **1 Nov 2026**, collected from the same party that pays the duty.
  - Sellers and platforms facilitating non-EU sales are "regarded as importers": they must provide data, pay or guarantee duties, and ensure product compliance.
  - New EU Customs Authority (EUCA) in Lille.
  - EU Customs Data Hub optional from 2031 and mandatory from 2034.

  Source: [Eunews 16 Sep 2026](https://www.eunews.it/en/2026/09/16/eu-parliament-gives-final-approval-to-new-rules-on-e-commerce-and-customs/)
- [Secondary] A €2 handling fee figure has circulated but is **not officially confirmed** in the legislation. — [Byrd blog](https://blog.getbyrd.com/en/de-minimis-eu); [Consilium customs union policy page](https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/policies/modernising-the-eu-customs-union/)

### Inferences
- **Per parcel vs. per item.** The Council's December 2025 wording has been widely reported as €3 "per item" in some places. Some advisers interpret it as per distinct tariff line or item category in a parcel. Others, such as Baker Tilly, say per parcel. **This conflict could not be resolved.** The vatcalc page and the Council small-parcels page both returned 403. Verify on the Commission DG TAXUD page before modelling. For a single-category bed linen parcel, both readings likely give €3.
- **VAT rules** [BACKGROUND, UNVERIFIED, standard since 1 July 2021]:
  - Import VAT is charged on all goods; the €22 exemption is gone.
  - IOSS can only be used for consignments of €150 or less. The seller charges NL 21% (or BE 21%, DE 19%) VAT at checkout and files monthly via IOSS.
  - For consignments above €150, standard import declaration and VAT apply at import. A €150+ duvet-plus-linen set from China therefore cannot use IOSS.
- **Model A is now hit by up to three per-parcel costs:**
  - the €3 duty
  - the handling fee (amount TBD, from about Nov 2026)
  - carrier or postal clearance fees

  Bulk import (Model B) pays the ad-valorem duty (12% or 3.7%, see section 4) once per shipment, on a customs value that excludes retail margin. For low-ticket items, the €3 flat duty can be cheaper than 12% ad valorem: 12% of a €20 customs value is €2.40. For higher-value items, the €3 is cheaper per parcel. The handling fee and the importer-of-record obligations remove most of the cost advantage, though.
- Bulk import into NL stock gives better control over compliance: labelling, GPSR, and UPV registration as importer. That matters because the reform makes the seller or platform responsible as importer.

### Gaps
- The exact handling fee amount (Commission delegated act) and whether NL started collecting it on 1 Nov 2026. Not found.
- The Dutch implementation and any separate national NL handling fee. No Dutch-specific source was found. Check Douane and the Belastingdienst.
- The €3 duty end date. It is "temporary" until the Customs Data Hub and full abolition of the €150 duty exemption (2028 was originally planned). Exact text not verified.

## 3. Sea and rail freight China to Rotterdam: FCL, LCL, transit, charges

### Takeaway
Spot ocean rates Shanghai–Rotterdam are about **USD 3,400 per 40ft** (Drewry WCI, 1 Oct 2026; Freightos FBX about USD 3,400/FEU, 30 Sep 2026). Rates have fallen for 12 consecutive weeks on weak demand and increased Suez/Red Sea transits. They remain well above pre-2024 levels because of Strait of Hormuz and Houthi uncertainty. I found no verified 2026 LCL per-CBM rates or rail rates.

### Cited Findings
- [INDEX] Drewry WCI, 1 Oct 2026:
  - Shanghai–Rotterdam fell 2% to **USD 3,399/40ft**.
  - Composite USD 4,434/40ft.
  - Asia–Europe rates have declined for 12 consecutive weeks.
  - Suez transits in week 39 were 68% above the same week last year.
  - Risks: Golden Week, Houthi activity, Strait of Hormuz disruptions.
  - Carriers are attempting late-October increases.

  Source: [Drewry WCI](https://www.drewry.co.uk/supply-chain-advisors/supply-chain-expertise/world-container-index-assessed-by-drewry)
- [INDEX] WCI Shanghai–Rotterdam earlier in September 2026: USD 3,997 (10 Sep) and USD 3,626 (17 Sep). — [DCN WCI 17 Sep 2026](https://www.thedcn.com.au/news/world-container-index-17-september-2026); [DCN WCI 1 Oct 2026](https://www.thedcn.com.au/news/world-container-index-1-october-2026)
- [INDEX] Freightos FBX11 Asia–N. Europe fell 9% to about **USD 3,400/FEU** (30 Sep 2026). — [Freightos 30 Sep 2026](https://www.freightos.com/freight-resources/china-us-truce-to-reduce-some-tariffs-and-likely-postpone-port-fees-september-30-2026-update/)

### Inferences
- **20ft vs. 40ft** [BACKGROUND, UNVERIFIED]: a 20ft is typically priced at about 55–65% of the 40ft rate, so roughly USD 1,900–2,300 now.
- **Capacity** [BACKGROUND, UNVERIFIED]: a 40ft HC holds about 76 m³. For uncompressed duvets that is only about 600–900 pieces. Vacuum-packed, it is several thousand. Bed linen sets are dense, so a 40ft could hold about 5,000–8,000 sets.
- **Per-unit freight** at about USD 3,400 per FEU is in the order of **USD 0.50–1.50 per bed linen set**. Compare about USD 4–5/kg for air linehaul alone. This is an inference from the indices above.
- **Additional typical charges** [BACKGROUND, UNVERIFIED]: origin THC and docs, destination THC at Rotterdam, delivery order fee, customs broker fee, and trucking to a 3PL.
- **Transit times** [BACKGROUND, UNVERIFIED]:
  - Port-to-port via Suez: about 30–35 days.
  - Via the Cape: about 40–50 days.
  - Door-to-door: add 1–2 weeks.
  - Rail China–Europe (to Duisburg, Małaszewicze or Tilburg): about 18–25 days, typically priced between sea and air.
- **LCL** [BACKGROUND, UNVERIFIED]: LCL is typically quoted per CBM (or per 1,000 kg, whichever is higher), plus CFS and handling charges at both ends. For a first order of about 5–15 m³, LCL is likely cheaper than FCL.

### Gaps

- No verified 2026 China–Europe rail rate per container or per CBM.
- No verified destination charges at Rotterdam (THC, DO fee) for 2026.

## 4. Customs duty (HS 6302, 9404, 6301), anti-dumping, import VAT, art. 23 licence, broker costs

### Takeaway
EU third-country (erga omnes) duty rates:
- **12%** for cotton bed linen (6302 31 00), confirmed on tariffnumber.com for 2026.
- **3.7%** for stuffed bedding (9404 90 pillows/cushions), confirmed via a TARIC-derived source. Duvets fall under 9404 40 10 (down) and 9404 40 90 (other); 3.7% is likely the rate for these too but was not directly confirmed.

No China-specific anti-dumping measure on 6302 was shown. Import VAT in NL is 21%. It can be moved to the VAT return with an **article 23 licence** (verleggingsregeling) for NL-established businesses that import regularly.

### Cited Findings
- [FACT, tariff database mirror] HS 6302 31 00 (cotton bed linen, not printed, not knitted) has EU third-country duty **12.0%** (erga omnes, 2026). No China anti-dumping or preference was shown. Preferences exist for Vietnam (0%), Singapore (0%) and Mercosur (10.6%). — [tariffnumber.com 63023100 (2026)](https://www.tariffnumber.com/2026/63023100)
- [FACT, tariff database mirror] Cushions under TARIC 9404 90 90 have EU duty **3.7%**. — [TheTradeHub TARIC 9404909000](https://www.thetradehub.eu/en/customs/taric/940490900080) (via search summary)
- [FACT] Heading 9404 structure (2026):
  - 9404 40: quilts, bedspreads, eiderdowns and duvets. 9404 40 10 is feather/down filled; 9404 40 90 is other.
  - 9404 90: other stuffed bedding such as pillows and cushions. 9404 90 10 is feather/down; 9404 90 90 is other.

  The tariffnumber page did not display the rates. — [tariffnumber.com 9404 (2026)](https://www.tariffnumber.com/2026/9404)
- [FACT] Article 23 licence (verleggingsregeling bij invoer):
  - Eligibility: an entrepreneur established in NL who regularly imports goods from non-EU countries and keeps separate records showing the import VAT.
  - Licensees must file VAT quarterly; annual filing is not allowed.
  - Apply in writing to the tax office. The application gives the VAT ID, type and value of goods, origin countries and frequency.

  Source: [Belastingdienst – Vergunning artikel 23 aanvragen](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/zakendoen_met_het_buitenland/zakendoen_buiten_de_eu/aangifte_doen_als_u_zakendoet_buiten_de_eu/aangifte_doen_als_u_goederen_importeert_uit_niet_eu_landen/vergunning_artikel_23_aanvragen); [Belastingdienst – Verleggingsregeling](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/internationaal/btw_voor_buitenlandse_ondernemers/goederen/importeren_vanuit_niet_eu_landen_naar_nederland/verleggingsregeling_bij_import_uit_niet_eu_landen)

### Inferences
- [BACKGROUND, UNVERIFIED] Other 6302 lines (6302 21 printed cotton, 6302 22 man-made fibre, 6302 10 knitted) are generally **12%** in the EU tariff.
- [BACKGROUND, UNVERIFIED] Blankets and throws under 6301 are mostly **12%** for 6301 30 cotton and 6301 40 synthetic. 6301 20 wool may differ. Verify in TARIC.
- [BACKGROUND, UNVERIFIED] Customs value is CIF: goods + freight + insurance to the EU border. Duty is calculated on the CIF value. Import VAT of 21% is calculated on CIF + duty.
- **Product-mix lever.** Duvets and pillows at 3.7% are far cheaper to import than covers and sheets at 12%. Duvet covers are 6302, not 9404.
- [BACKGROUND, UNVERIFIED] Customs broker or declarant fees in NL are typically about EUR 50–150 per import declaration, plus extra per additional HS line. Get quotes.
- [BACKGROUND, UNVERIFIED] Products from Vietnam enter at 0% under the EU–Vietnam FTA, if they meet the origin rules (above). This could be a strategic alternative to China for 6302 items.

### Gaps
- Direct confirmation of the duty rates for 9404 40 90 (duvets) and 6301 sub-lines. The pages either did not show rates or were not fetched. Check the [EU TARIC consultation](https://ec.europa.eu/taxation_customs/dds2/taric/taric_consultation.jsp) or [Access2Markets](https://trade.ec.europa.eu/access-to-markets/).
- No current EU anti-dumping measure on Chinese bed linen was found. The tariffnumber page showed none, but TARIC itself was not queried.

## 5. Regulations: textile labelling, GPSR, REACH, UPV Textiel (NL EPR), EU textile EPR, DPP, green claims, OEKO-TEX, flammability, packaging EPR

### Takeaway
Three items are confirmed by primary sources:
- **NL UPV Textiel** covers producers and importers established in NL. The **2026 fee via Stichting UPV Textiel is EUR 0.24/kg** placed on the Dutch market. Report the weight annually by 1 April.
- The **EU-wide textile EPR** (Directive (EU) 2025/1892) entered into force on 16 Oct 2025. Member states must have schemes running within 30 months, so by about April 2028. Micro-enterprises get 42 months.
- From **27 Sep 2026**, the ECGT/EmpCo rules apply in NL. Generic green claims and self-made sustainability labels are banned. Only independently certified labels are allowed.

Labelling, GPSR and REACH obligations are well established, but their sources were not fetched in this session.

### Cited Findings
- [FACT] Stichting UPV Textiel:
  - Serves textile producers and importers established in NL.
  - **Textielbeheerbijdrage 2026: EUR 0.24 per kg** of textile placed on the Dutch market.
  - Registration has three steps: registration form, signing the TBBO agreement, and submitting a weight projection before 1 April.
  - Actual weights for the previous calendar year are reported by 1 April each year, in kg (not pieces).
  - Small companies receive one annual invoice in September; larger companies are invoiced quarterly.

  Source: [Stichting UPV Textiel – Producenten](https://www.stichtingupvtextiel.nl/producenten/)
- [FACT] The regulator is ILT, the Inspectie Leefomgeving en Transport. — [ILT – UPV textiel](https://www.ilent.nl/onderwerpen/producentenverantwoordelijkheid/upv-textiel)
- [FACT/secondary] The revised Waste Framework Directive, **Directive (EU) 2025/1892**:
  - Published in the OJ on 26 Sep 2025; in force on **16 Oct 2025**.
  - Member states must set up textile EPR schemes within **30 months** (42 months for micro-enterprises).
  - Covers producers placing textiles on the EU consumer market, including **online sellers and non-EU entities**.

  Sources: [UKFT](https://ukft.org/revised-waste-framework-directive-sep25/); [EXPRA](https://expra.eu/2025/12/20/eu-publishes-revised-waste-framework-directive-mandatory-textile-epr-and-food-waste-targets-enter-into-force/); [Brubru 2025/1892](https://brubru.beresol.eu/eucanon/2025-1892_wfd_textiles/)
- [FACT] ACM, new rules from **27 Sep 2026** (implementing the Empowering Consumers for the Green Transition Directive):
  - Generic claims such as "groen", "milieuvriendelijk" or "ecologisch" are only allowed when they meet strict European or national standards.
  - "Eigen bedrijfslabels zonder onafhankelijke controle zijn niet meer toegestaan" (own company labels without independent checks are no longer allowed).
  - Standardised warranty information is required.
  - Transitional flexibility applies for old stock.

  Sources: [ACM – Vanaf 27 september 2026 strengere eisen](https://www.acm.nl/nl/publicaties/vanaf-27-september-2026-strengere-eisen-aan-duurzaamheidsclaims); [ACM – Leidraad duurzaamheidsclaims](https://www.acm.nl/nl/publicaties/leidraad-duurzaamheidsclaims-0); [ACM – old stock explanation](https://www.acm.nl/nl/publicaties/toelichting-op-de-toepassing-van-de-eu-richtlijn-duurzaamheidsclaims-bij-oude-voorraden)

### Inferences
- **UPV cost per item.** A 1 kg bed linen set costs about EUR 0.24 in UPV fee. A 2 kg duvet costs about EUR 0.48. This is negligible per item, but the registration and reporting work is mandatory once the business imports as an NL entity (Model B).
- **Model A and the UPV.** It is ambiguous who is the "producer" when an NL webshop sells goods shipped directly from a Chinese factory. Under the new EU WFD, non-EU sellers must appoint an authorised representative. If the NL entity is the seller, it is most likely the producer.
- **Wording to avoid from 27 Sep 2026.** Avoid claims like "duurzaam beddengoed" or "eco" without substantiation.
  - **OEKO-TEX STANDARD 100** is an independent third-party certification and can be referenced. Only use it for certified articles, with the certificate number and label check. It is a harmful-substances test label, not a sustainability label, so do not present it as "eco".
  - Self-created "planet-friendly" badges are banned.
- [BACKGROUND, UNVERIFIED] **Textile Labelling Regulation (EU) 1007/2011:**
  - Fibre composition must be on a durable label or marking, using the standardised fibre names (e.g. "katoen", "polyester").
  - It must appear in the official language(s) of the member state where the product is sold: Dutch for NL; Dutch/French (and German) for BE; German for DE.
  - The composition must also be shown **on the webshop before purchase**.
  - Filling of duvets/pillows: composition of textile parts is required. Down/feather has separate labelling practice (EN 12934).
  - Source to verify: [EUR-Lex 1007/2011](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2011/1007/oj)
- [BACKGROUND, UNVERIFIED] **GPSR (Regulation (EU) 2023/988)**, applicable since 13 Dec 2024:
  - Requires an EU-based responsible economic operator. For Model B, the NL importer is that operator.
  - Requirements: product identification (type/batch), the operator's name and contact details on the product or packaging, an internal risk assessment and technical documentation, and incident and recall processes.
  - Online listings must show the operator info, product identifiers, safety warnings and images.
  - Source to verify: [EUR-Lex 2023/988](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2023/988/oj)
- [BACKGROUND, UNVERIFIED] **REACH Annex XVII:**
  - Azo dyes that release listed aromatic amines are restricted in textiles in skin contact (entry 43).
  - Entry 72 sets CMR substance limits in textiles and clothing.
  - Other relevant entries: nickel, PFAS restrictions (PFHxA, adopted 2024), and SVHC information duty (>0.1% w/w).
  - Practical step: require supplier test reports (e.g. SGS/Intertek), or OEKO-TEX 100 certificates as supporting evidence.
- [BACKGROUND, UNVERIFIED] **Flammability:** there is no general EU mandatory flammability standard for bed linen or duvets sold to consumers. GPSR general safety applies. Contract/hospitality markets have their own norms.
- [BACKGROUND, UNVERIFIED] **Digital Product Passport (ESPR, Regulation (EU) 2024/1781):** textiles are a priority product group. A delegated act on textiles was expected around 2027, with DPP obligations about 18 months later. Not yet applicable to bed linen in 2026.
- [BACKGROUND, UNVERIFIED] **Packaging EPR NL (Afvalfonds Verpakkingen):** companies placing more than 50,000 kg of packaging on the NL market per year pay the Afvalbeheersbijdrage and report. Below that threshold only registration or a report applies. The EU PPWR (2025/40) applies from Aug 2026, with phased obligations.

### Gaps
- Confirmation that bed linen, duvets and pillows fall within NL UPV Textiel scope. The Stichting page refers to Rijkswaterstaat's FAQ; the scope covers clothing and "huishoudtextiel" but this was not fetched. Also not confirmed whether filled products (9404) are in scope.
- The NL legislative transposition timeline of Directive 2025/1892 (adapting UPV Textiel). Not found.
- 2026 Afvalfonds tariffs and thresholds, and current PPWR application details. Not fetched.
- BE and DE textile EPR status. BE: Fost Plus/Valipac do not cover textiles; Flanders has textile EPR plans. Not researched.

## 6. Consumer law (NL/BE): withdrawal, return costs, pre-order disclosure, deposits

### Takeaway
I did not fetch primary sources for this question in this session. The points below are standard EU consumer-rights rules (Consumer Rights Directive 2011/83/EU, implemented in Dutch Civil Code Book 7). They should be verified with ACM ConsuWijzer and Thuiswinkel.

### Cited Findings
- [FACT] From 27 Sep 2026, ACM requires standardised legal and commercial warranty information for consumers (ECGT implementation). — [ACM 27 Sep 2026](https://www.acm.nl/nl/publicaties/vanaf-27-september-2026-strengere-eisen-aan-duurzaamheidsclaims)

### Inferences
- **[BACKGROUND, UNVERIFIED] Withdrawal right:**
  - 14-day withdrawal period from receipt of the goods.
  - The seller must refund within 14 days of withdrawal, including the standard outbound shipping cost.
  - The consumer may be charged the direct return costs only if this was disclosed before purchase.
  - Hygiene exemptions apply only to sealed goods that were unsealed. Bed linen and duvets are generally **not** exempt unless sealed for hygiene reasons; pillows are debated.
- **[BACKGROUND, UNVERIFIED] Delivery time and pre-orders:**
  - Without an agreed time, the statutory maximum delivery period is **30 days**. Pre-order (Model A) is allowed, but the expected delivery period must be clearly stated before purchase.
  - **Deposits:** NL law (Art. 7:26 BW) limits a required advance payment for consumer purchases to max. **50%** of the price. Requiring 100% prepayment for a pre-order is not enforceable, though consumers may choose to pay in full.
  - Many Thuiswinkel members follow the Thuiswinkel Algemene Voorwaarden.
- **[BACKGROUND, UNVERIFIED] The "Bestelknop met betalingsverplichting"** wording (EU Omnibus, price indications) also applies.

### Gaps
- Primary-source confirmation of the 50% advance-payment limit, the 30-day delivery default and return-cost rules was not fetched. Check the [ACM ConsuWijzer](https://www.consuwijzer.nl/) and [Thuiswinkel](https://www.thuiswinkel.org/) pages.
- Belgian (FOD Economie) and German specifics were not checked. In DE there is no 50% prepayment cap; check BGB rules.

## 7. Fulfilment in NL: 3PL costs per order, storage, PostNL/DHL parcel rates

### Takeaway
NL 3PL benchmarks for 2026:
- Pick & pack about **EUR 2–4 per order**.
- Pallet storage about **EUR 5–25 per pallet per month**.
- Inbound/outbound about EUR 2–8 per pallet.
- Domestic shipping about EUR 4–7 per parcel at contract rates.

Some all-in offers start at EUR 4.95 per parcel. PostNL's **standard (non-contract) tariff to Belgium** is EUR 13.55 (0–2 kg) and EUR 20.55 (2–5 kg). Contract rates are much lower.

### Cited Findings
- [AGGREGATOR] NL 3PL benchmarks (claimed source: 500+ warehouse RFQs from 45+ NL 3PLs, updated Q1 2026):
  - Pallet storage about EUR 5–15/pallet/month (range EUR 5–25).
  - Pick & pack EUR 2–4 per parcel.
  - Domestic shipping EUR 4–7.
  - Inbound/outbound EUR 2–8 per euro pallet.

### Inferences
- **Per-order cost estimate for Model B:** pick & pack EUR 2–4 + packaging EUR 0.30–1 + parcel EUR 4–7 (NL) ≈ **EUR 6.50–12 per order**, plus storage.
  - Storage example: a pallet of duvets holds about 40–80 pieces, so EUR 10/month is about EUR 0.15–0.25 per piece per month. A pallet of bed linen sets holds about 150–300 sets.
  - Bulky duvets often fall into a larger parcel size class, raising the carrier price.
  - BE delivery from an NL warehouse costs more (contract rates of about EUR 6–9 are typical; inference) unless the 3PL has volume rates.
- **Model A comparison:** each consumer parcel from China carries:
  - air or e-commerce-line freight on volumetric weight
  - the €3 duty
  - the upcoming handling fee
  - 7–25 days of transit

  Returns then go to an NL address, so a local returns address or 3PL is needed anyway.
- Named 3PLs for quotes: Monta, Bleckmann, and others. Their pricing is not published (quote-based).
