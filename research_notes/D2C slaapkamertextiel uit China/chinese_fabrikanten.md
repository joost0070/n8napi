# Chinese manufacturers of mid-to-premium bed linen: clusters, ready-stock terms, prices, certification and vetting (status Oct 2026)

Research date: 2026-10-04. Most supplier figures come from live marketplace listings (Alibaba, Made-in-China) and supplier or sourcing-agent blogs. Treat these as indicative quotes, not transaction prices. Listing prices are usually "from" prices, often for the smallest size or the cheapest construction. Undated web pages are marked "undated".

## 1. Supplier clusters: what each is good at

### Takeaway
Nantong (Jiangsu) is by far the main hub for finished bedding: duvet covers, sheets, quilts and pillows. It has two huge wholesale markets (Dieshiqiao and Zhihao) and thousands of factories in Haimen. Shaoxing/Keqiao (Zhejiang) is the hub for fabric, including stock fabric, and for sherpa/flannel/fleece blankets. Hangzhou and the wider Zhejiang region host several linen and lyocell bedding specialists. I found almost no sourced evidence that Ningbo or Guangzhou/Foshan are bedding clusters.

### Inferences
- Nantong is the natural starting point for duvet covers, fitted sheets and pillowcases, and also for duvets and pillows, which are sold as quilt and pillow "inserts".
- Shaoxing/Keqiao is the place to find ready-stock fabric and sherpa/fleece throws or sherpa covers.
- Zhejiang (Hangzhou, Kaihua) is relevant for washed linen and lyocell.
- Ningbo, Hangzhou (outside the linen/sherpa makers) and Guangzhou/Foshan did not show up as bedding clusters in any source I found. Guangzhou is apparel-fabric focused. Ningbo is mainly relevant as an export port and for general trading companies (inference, not sourced).

- I found no sourced data on down/feather duvet clusters. Anhui and Zhejiang are commonly cited, but I did not verify this.

## 2. Ready stock (现货), MOQs, fabric MOQs, private label, samples and lead times

### Takeaway
Ready-stock finished bedding is widely available, mainly in Nantong and on 1688. This includes dropshipping from a single unit (一件代发). However, that stock is mostly built for the Chinese domestic market: Chinese sizes, Chinese labels, and mostly four-piece sets (四件套).

For export private label, the realistic floor is about 100–500 sets per colour per size, and only if the factory uses stock or in-line fabric. Custom-dyed fabric triggers the dye-lot MOQ, which typically means about 3,000 m per colour or 500–1,000 pcs per colour. That is exactly the wall Bline hit.

### Inferences: how ready-stock fabrics and stock programmes avoid Bline's refusals
Bline's September 2026 inquiries were refused at 500 pcs (MOQ 800 per colour, 3,000 m fabric). This is the custom-dye MOQ. One dye-lot of about 3,000 m yields roughly 800+ duvet cover sets, since a 240x220 duvet cover plus 2 pillowcases needs about 3.5 m of 2.4–2.8 m wide fabric (estimate). That is why the factory quotes 800 per colour.

Ways around it:
1. **Pick from the factory's in-line or stock colours.** Mills in Nantong and Keqiao keep greige and stock-dyed sateen and percale in popular shades such as white, ivory, grey and sage. The cutting room then only needs 100–300 sets per colour per size. Per BeddingTextilePro this also shortens the lead time to 5–8 weeks.
2. **Buy stock fabric yourself in Keqiao** (1,000 m or less in some cases) and have a Nantong CMT workshop sew it.
3. **Use a factory's ODM stock programme**, adding only your own woven label and packaging. This is the 100 sets per colour per size model.
4. **Order 1688 现货 sets** and relabel them, keeping the size issue in mind (see section 4).
5. **For sherpa**, use stock-coloured sherpa. The dye MOQ is about 500 kg per colour, so finished-throw MOQs of 100–200 pcs only work on stock colours.

- I found no hard data on sample costs. A common practice is that samples are charged and refunded against the bulk order, but this is not verified in my sources.
- I could not extract exact ready-stock colour cards or stock depths per factory from public sources. This needs direct inquiry.

## 4. European sizes from Chinese stock

### Takeaway
Export factories routinely make EU sizes to order: 240x220 duvet covers, and 160/180x200 fitted sheets with a deep corner. Ready stock, however, is mostly in Chinese domestic sizes or US/UK sizes. EU sizes are only "in stock" at exporters that run European programmes. Some 220x240 stock listings exist, mostly microfiber.

### Cited Findings
- BeddingTextilePro offers "market-specific sizing (US, UK, EU, Australian, Middle East)" but does not list EU metric dimensions. — [BeddingTextilePro](https://www.beddingtextilepro.com/private-label-bedding)
- Nantong Oulai Trading lists a 220x240 duvet cover set with MOQ 5 sets (microfiber). This confirms that some EU-size stock exists. — [Made-in-China microfiber duvet set](https://www.made-in-china.com/products-search/hot-china-products/Microfiber_Duvet_Cover_Set.html)
- An overview of European bedding sizes and custom sizing (useful for spec sheets) is available. — [von Schuetz bed linen sizes](https://vonschuetz.com/en/bedding-sizes/)

### Inferences
- Chinese domestic four-piece sets typically use 200x230 or 220x240 duvet covers and flat sheets rather than fitted sheets. Fitted sheets come in 1.5/1.8/2.0 m bed widths with shallower corners than the Dutch 30 cm hoek. So 1.5 m ≈ 150x200 is not 160x200 (based on general market knowledge, not verified in a fetched source).
- A Dutch D2C brand should therefore expect to order EU sizes as a (small) production run, ideally on stock fabric, rather than buying them off the shelf.
- 220x240 duvet covers are the most likely EU-compatible size to be found in domestic stock.

## 5. Certifications and quality risks

### Takeaway
OEKO-TEX Standard 100 is common among Nantong and Zhejiang exporters and can be verified in about 2 minutes via OEKO-TEX Label Check. GOTS is verified via the GOTS public database plus a Transaction Certificate for each shipment. BSCI gives an A–E grade.

The main quality risks are:
- thread-count inflation through multi-ply yarn
- colour fastness
- shrinkage (dimensional stability)

All of these are checked by standard lab tests.

### Cited Findings
- **OEKO-TEX verification:**
  - Use Label Check at oeko-tex.com, or scan the QR code, then enter the certificate number. The result is "valid", "expired" or "withdrawn".
  - A valid result shows the company name, product class, issue and expiry dates, and scope.
  - Certificates are valid for one year and can be renewed at the earliest 90 days before expiry.
  — [OEKO-TEX Standard 100](https://www.oeko-tex.com/en/our-standards/oeko-tex-standard-100/); [Hohenstein guide (PDF)](https://www.hohenstein.de/fileadmin/user_upload/Downloads/Tools_and_Guides/OEKO-TEX_Guide_How_to_Read_OEKO-TEX_Certificates_and_Check_Their_Validity.pdf); [ComplianceGate](https://www.compliancegate.com/oeko-tex-standard-100/)
- **GOTS verification:**
  - Use the license number in the GOTS public database (global-standard.org, "Certified Suppliers").
  - Check that the scope covers the right production stages and that the facility address matches the factory.
  - Ask for a Transaction Certificate for each shipment.
  — [GOTS certified suppliers database](https://global-standard.org/de/oeffentliche-datenbanken/certifiedsuppliers); [newway-apparel 2026 guide](https://www.newway-apparel.com/blog/how-to-verify-china-factory-sustainability-certifications-2026.html)
- **amfori BSCI:**
  - The audit has 81 questions and ends in a grade from A to E, on a 2-year cycle.
  - Grades below B require a follow-up audit within 2–12 months.
  - Findings are accessible to amfori members and audited suppliers.
  — [amfori BSCI](https://www.amfori.org/amfori-bsci/); [Eurofins](https://www.eurofins.com/assurance/resources/articles/what-is-amfori-bsci-audit-requirement-benefits-and-process/)
- **Thread-count inflation:**
  - 300 real threads with 2-ply yarn get labelled 600; with 3-ply, 900.
  - Single-ply weaves physically top out at about 500–600 TC.
  - The US FTC has warned against this practice.
  — [Boll & Branch TC guide 2026](https://www.bollandbranch.com/guides/thread-count-explained-the-complete-guide-to-buying-better-sheets-2026/); [Matteola single vs multi-ply](https://matteola.com/blogs/resources/single-ply-vs-multi-ply-cotton-sheets-why-it-matters-more-than-thread-count)
- **Standard bedding tests:** fibre content, pH, colour fastness (washing, rubbing/crocking, perspiration, light, saliva), banned azo amines, dimensional change after washing, tensile/tear/seam strength, pilling, thread count and fabric weight. — [Testex bedding testing](https://www.testextextile.com/expert-interpretation-quality-and-testing-of-bedding-sets/)
- **Shrinkage methods:** ISO 6330 (domestic washing), ISO 5077 (dimensional change) and AATCC 135/150. Colour fastness is graded on a 1–5 scale (1–8 for light). — [Ninghow shrinkage test](https://ninghow.com/blog/fabric-shrinkage-test-buyers-ask/); [fabric-supplier.com colourfastness](https://fabric-supplier.com/what-is-colorfastness-fabric-test-standards/)

### Inferences
- In the spec sheet, define TC as single-ply (e.g. "400 TC, 60s x 60s single-ply combed long-staple cotton").
- Set targets such as:
  - shrinkage ≤3% after ISO 6330 at 60 °C
  - colour fastness to washing ≥4
  - rubbing ≥3–4
- Ask for an OEKO-TEX certificate whose scope covers the finished article or the specific fabric, not just "some" fabric. Then verify it with Label Check.
- Smell (from formaldehyde finishes or packaging) is covered by OEKO-TEX limits and a sniff test at inspection.

### Gaps
- I found no sourced 2026 price list for a single bedding lab-test package from SGS, Intertek or QIMA. Typical colour fastness plus shrinkage plus composition tests are believed to cost a few hundred USD, but this is unverified.

### Inferences
Suggested fast vetting path:
1. Shortlist via Alibaba, Made-in-China or 1688. On Alibaba/Made-in-China, filter for Nantong/Zhejiang, Verified/Audited Supplier, OEKO-TEX and EU export markets.
2. Verify certificates online, using OEKO-TEX Label Check and the GOTS database.
3. Order paid samples in the target EU size, with stock fabric colour cards.
4. Lab-test the samples (shrinkage and colour fastness).
5. Book a pre-shipment inspection at AQL 2.5 for about US$200–350.
6. Optionally, use a Nantong-based sourcing agent, or visit Dieshiqiao or Heimtextil (Jan 2027) to meet Nantong exporters.

## 7. Examples of Chinese bedding factories serving EU brands and dropshipping to the EU

### Takeaway
Many Nantong and Zhejiang exporters list Western Europe as a market and offer EU sizing and private label. I found no verified public evidence of a Chinese bedding factory that runs EU-warehouse D2C dropshipping for brands. The realistic model is small private-label stock-and-ship orders, or 1688/agent consolidation.

### Inferences
- For a Dutch D2C brand, the fastest route is likely the following:
  - 100–300 sets per colour per size
  - on stock sateen, percale or washed-linen fabric
  - from a Nantong (cotton) or Zhejiang (linen/Tencel) exporter
  - with woven labels and packaging sourced at Dieshiqiao
  - shipped by sea, or by rail or air for the first drop, to an NL 3PL
- Plan about 5–8 weeks production plus 2–6 weeks transit.
