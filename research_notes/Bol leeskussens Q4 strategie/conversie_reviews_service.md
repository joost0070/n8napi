# Conversion-rate and review levers on bol.com for a small private-label FBR seller (2026)

Scope: bol.com NL/BE, 2025-2026. Seller context used for inferences: private label "Bline", 70-80 euro home product, FBR (own shipping) via 3PL eFreight (same-day dispatch before cut-off, next-day delivery), few reviews, kwaliteitsscore ~69, one cancellation and one customer case in week 37, clean in week 38.

Legend used throughout: **[bol rule]** = documented on partnerplatform.bol.com / bol.com; **[agency]** = agency or tool-vendor opinion/claim; **[non-bol data]** = generic e-commerce research not specific to bol; **[older]** = material from before 2025.

## Key Question 1: What does bol publish about the "productinformatie score" / content score and its link to findability?

### Takeaway
bol publishes that an algorithm scores each partner's product information per **product group** (not per item) and that the partner with the highest score in a product group gets their content shown on the shared product page; findability is driven primarily by the correct product group, complete product specifications (which feed filters), and title/description quality. bol does not publish the scoring formula, field weights, or a numeric "content score" threshold.

### Cited Findings
- **[bol rule]** "An algorithm evaluates partners' product information, resulting in a score per partner, and the product information from the partner with the highest score automatically appears online on a product page." The score "does not look at individual items, but at product group level"; poorer content on some items in a product group lowers the score, good content raises it. — [Partnerplatform: Productinformatie schrijven / hoe jouw content meer kans maakt](https://partnerplatform.bol.com/nl/hulp-nodig/productinformatie/hoe-jouw-content-meer-kans-maakt-om-online-te-verschijnen/)
- **[bol rule]** The page does **not** disclose which fields carry weight, minimum image counts, or video requirements; it links to separate title/description guidelines. — [same page](https://partnerplatform.bol.com/nl/hulp-nodig/productinformatie/hoe-jouw-content-meer-kans-maakt-om-online-te-verschijnen/)
- **[bol rule]** Findability: "The first step in improving article findability is choosing the right product group and product specifications, as articles are assigned a place in the shop and the right keywords are linked based on the product group. Product specifications form the basis for filters on the website; the more product specifications known for your article, the more accurately customers can search for it." — [Partnerplatform: Zo maakt bol jouw artikelen vindbaar](https://partnerplatform.bol.com/en/need-help/productinformation/findability-of-your-items/)
- **[bol rule]** Image rules: file 30 KB-10 MB; 500x500 to 6000x6000 px; zoom only works at >=1200x1200 px; first image = product only, neutral/white background, no shadow, unpacked, straight-on, filling the frame; no models, text, logos, watermarks or illustrations on the first image; lifestyle/sfeer images allowed as secondary images; images may not contain discount labels, promotional text, comparisons or reviews; images must be royalty-free (not from Google Images or other sellers' pages). — [Partnerplatform: Deze regels gelden voor je afbeeldingen](https://partnerplatform.bol.com/nl/idp/regels-voor-afbeeldingen)
- **[bol rule]** "As of July 1, 2025, bol no longer enforces image guidelines (since these standards aim to improve visibility rather than protect consumers), but restrictions on self-promotional content in images remain enforced." — [Partnerplatform: regels voor afbeeldingen](https://partnerplatform.bol.com/nl/idp/regels-voor-afbeeldingen)
- **[bol rule]** Video: sales partners can upload video in the seller account under "Productinformatie bewerken" in the block for higher conversion; 1 MB-1 GB; formats .mkv .mp4 .flv .avi .mpg .mpeg .mov .wmv .webm; converted to 16:9; one video per article shown, the last uploaded overwrites; filename without spaces/special characters; no brand-owner-only restriction stated. — [Partnerplatform: Video's toevoegen aan je productinformatie](https://partnerplatform.bol.com/nl/idp/videos-toevoegen); mirrored on the supplier portal where the upload sits under a heading literally called "Increase conversion" — [Leveranciersplatform: add videos](https://leveranciers.bol.com/en/need-help/content/add-videos-to-your-products/)
- **[bol rule]** The seller account has a "Conversie analyse" report (conversion = % of customers who view the item and then order). — [Partnerplatform: Conversie analyse in je verkoopaccount](https://partnerplatform.bol.com/en/need-help/offer/conversion-analysis-in-your-seller-account/)
- **[agency]** Title: 70-character limit, format "[Brand] [Series] [Product group] - [Size] - [Quantity/Feature]", first ~35 characters visible on mobile; description should use headings, bold, bullets; avoid "goedkoop", "gratis", URLs, ALL CAPS, prices, delivery times in copy. — [Boloo: Zo optimaliseer je je bol.com productpagina's](https://www.boloo.co/bibliotheek/zo-optimaliseer-je-je-bolcom-productpagina-s)
- **[agency]** "At least 4 images to show your product well; consumer attention is best maintained with approximately 6 images"; add at least 2 images from other angles and at least 1 image showing scale/proportion. — [Fotofy: productfoto's voor bol.com](https://fotofy.nl/productfotos-voor-bol-com-laten-maken/) (matches the pre-July-2025 bol image guideline wording)
- **[bol rule, older]** bol category "Content richtlijnen" PDFs (April 2022) exist per product format but only for specific categories (e.g. monitors, storage). — [Partnerplatform content richtlijnen PDF](https://partnerplatform.bol.com/content/uploads/2022/09/Content-richtlijnen-format-Netwerk.pdf)

### Inferences
- For a private-label EAN with a single seller, the "highest score wins the page" mechanism is moot; the practical lever is completeness (all specs filled, correct product group) because specs drive filter visibility and keyword mapping.
- Because image enforcement stopped 1 July 2025, secondary images can now carry infographics/text (still no self-promotion, prices or delivery claims), which is where agencies put USP call-outs for home products.
- The zoom threshold (1200x1200) is a concrete, checkable content requirement for a 70-80 euro tactile product.

### Gaps
- bol does not publish how the productinformatie score is computed, nor any numeric target; I found no bol page stating that a higher content score directly raises search rank (only that the highest-scoring partner's content is shown and that specs/product group drive findability).
- No bol page specifying maximum video length or where the video appears in the image carousel; the Boloo "12 seconds / 1152x250" figures look like ad-banner specs, not product video specs, and should not be relied on.

## Key Question 2: What conversion uplift do agencies report from video, more images, review counts on bol (numbers where possible)?

### Takeaway
No bol-published or bol-specific A/B numbers for video or image count exist in the sources found; agency numbers are recycled generic e-commerce statistics. The most bol-specific numbers come from review-mail tool vendors (BolKit, Bolmate) on review yield per order and the sales gap between 0-5 and 20+ reviews, and these are vendor estimates, not audited studies.

### Cited Findings
- **[agency, generic]** Boloo claims "high-quality photos give a 94% higher conversion rate", "multiple quality images can raise conversion by 250%", "93% of consumers say visual appearance is a deciding factor", "73% of visitors who watch product videos end up buying", "60%+ of shoppers buy faster when a page has customer videos", "89% of consumers want more brand video in 2024". No bol data source is given for any of these. — [Boloo productpagina optimalisatie](https://www.boloo.co/bibliotheek/zo-optimaliseer-je-je-bolcom-productpagina-s)
- **[agency, generic]** Sellerly repeats "visual content can raise conversion up to 35%; brands investing in visual content see up to 7x higher conversion" without a bol source. — [Sellerly: Hoe verhoog je je conversie op Bol.com](https://sellerly.nl/blog/hoe-verhoog-je-je-conversie-op-bol-com)
- **[agency]** MarktMentor: on bol, images are "the first and often the only thing" customers look at, "especially in the bol app where the description is almost hidden", so additional images are meant to raise conversion which in turn raises category position/visibility. — [MarktMentor: optimaliseren listing bol](https://www.marktmentor.nl/blog/optimaliseren-listing-bol)
- **[agency, vendor]** BolKit 2026 benchmark: products with "20+ reviews and 4+ stars average" sell roughly "2-3x" more than products with 0-5 reviews at equal price; reaching the 20+ review threshold is estimated at +10-20% revenue per product category; review-mail conversion typically 3-8% of mails, best case 1 review per 7 mails (~14%); at 10-20 orders/month expect only ~0.5-1 extra review/month, at 200 orders/month 6-16 extra reviews/month; first results after 2-3 weeks, reliable data after 6-8 weeks; review mails sent 7-14 days after delivery; bol moderation adds 1-3 days. — [BolKit: review-conversie benchmark 2026](https://bolkit.nl/blog/bol-com-review-conversie-benchmark-2026)
- **[agency, vendor]** Bolmate: best-performing templates yield ~1 review per 7 orders (14.93% click rate); platform average 1 review per 31 orders. — [Bolmate: Hoe krijg je meer reviews op bol](https://bolmate.nl/hoe-meer-bol-reviews/)
- **[agency]** Boloo review guides: "products with at least 5-10 reviews already have significantly higher conversion than products without"; "products with at least 10 reviews generate up to 52% more conversion than products without" (no primary source given); "aim for a minimum of 15-20 reviews per product"; bol's algorithm "weighs number, average rating and recency of reviews"; "20 reviews at 4.5 typically beats 50 reviews at 3.8; minimum 4.0 stars recommended". — [Boloo: Bol.com reviews](https://boloo.co/blog/bol-com-reviews); [Boloo: Bol review 2026](https://boloo.co/blog/bol-review)
- **[agency]** Storbit (2026): product reviews are shared across all sellers of an EAN and therefore do **not** decide the koopblok; the seller rating (minimum 8.0 average over last 3 months expected) is separate from the kwaliteitsscore. — [Storbit: Koopblok winnen op bol 2026](https://storbit.nl/blog/koopblok-winnen-bol)
- **[bol rule]** bol's own statement about video is limited to placing the upload button in a block labelled "Increase conversion". — [Leveranciersplatform: add videos](https://leveranciers.bol.com/en/need-help/content/add-videos-to-your-products/)

### Inferences
- With few reviews and low monthly volume, the realistic Q4 gain is moving from 0-5 to 10-20 reviews, which the vendor benchmarks place at the steepest part of the curve; at ~3-8% yield per order the seller needs roughly 150-300 delivered orders (or an above-average template + insert) to add ~10 reviews, so review-mail setup must start immediately, not in November.
- Video and image uplift numbers should be presented to the seller as "direction, not magnitude"; the only bol-verifiable metric is the seller's own conversion report before/after.

### Gaps
- No bol-published A/B data on video, image count or review count vs conversion on bol; no Emerce/Twinkle articles with bol-specific uplift numbers surfaced in searches.
- Generic secondary support (Spiegel Research Center, Bazaarvoice) was not fetched within the call budget; report writer should label any such figure as non-bol.

## Key Question 3: Which review-gathering practices are allowed on bol in 2026 and which are penalised?

### Takeaway
Since 1 July 2024 (Digital Services Act driven) any compensated review (free product, discount, gift card, test panels) is prohibited and bol will remove such reviews; bol sends its own review invitations to opted-in customers. A neutral request for an honest review inside the mandatory invoice/order email is treated as allowed by agencies and Sellerly, while bol's own review page says sellers may not contact customers to request reviews, so this is a grey zone to flag.

### Cited Findings
- **[bol rule]** bol emails review invitations to customers who consented to marketing; bol decides which products get an invitation via undisclosed logic that does not consider previous ratings. When a customer clicks to review, bol checks order history and awards the label "heeft dit artikel gekocht". Sellers/bol employees may not review articles they sell. Sponsored reviews (compensation via free products, discounts, gift cards) are prohibited. The bol page also states sellers "cannot contact customers requesting reviews". Reviews may not contain only remarks about price/shipping/seller, mentions of discounts/campaigns/price differences, profanity, or links to other sites. bol moderates authenticity and content; reviews appear within ~24 hours up to 3 days; bol may remove reviews without notice. — [Partnerplatform: Product reviews / productbeoordelingen](https://partnerplatform.bol.com/nl/idp/productbeoordelingen)
- **[bol rule via agency]** Rule change dated 1 July 2024: "Reviews for which the writer has received compensation, such as money or free products, can no longer be posted" (ends test panels and compensated programs), motivated by the DSA. "Sellers may request honest reviews from customers provided the request doesn't constitute an extra email beyond standard communications. Sending an email with an invoice is not considered an extra email." — [Sellerly: Gesponsorde reviews niet langer toegestaan](https://sellerly.nl/en/blog/gesponsorde-reviews-niet-langer-toegestaan-op-bol)
- **[bol rule, older]** bol removed the "Customer reviews" service standard from the prestatiescore (seller rating no longer part of the score). — [Partnerplatform: Customer Reviews service standard removed](https://partnerplatform.bol.com/en/nadp/customer-reviews-service-standard-removed-from-performance-score)
- **[agency, vendor]** Bolmate 2026: allowed = review request combined with functional order communication (invoice mail), neutral wording ("eerlijke review"), direct review link, QR code/insert in the package pointing to the review page; not allowed = discounts/gifts for reviews, buying reviews, asking only for 5 stars, pressure, standalone review-only emails ("extra contact"). bol itself sends automatic seller-rating requests 3-14 days post-delivery (opt-in dependent), separate from product reviews. — [Bolmate: Hoe krijg je meer reviews op bol](https://bolmate.nl/hoe-meer-bol-reviews/)
- **[agency]** Boloo: "you may not ask buyers to write a positive review, but you can remind them of the opportunity; a neutral 'we appreciate all feedback on bol.com' is allowed as long as it never includes compensation"; buying reviews is a violation. — [Boloo: Bol reviews kopen? Risico's & legale alternatieven](https://boloo.co/en/blog/bol-reviews-kopen); [Buddli: Bol reviews kopen, mag dat?](https://www.buddli.nl/blogs/bol-reviews-kopen-mag-dat)
- **[agency, older 2022]** Boloo's 2022 two-email approach (thank-you/tips mail, then a review-request mail days later) predates the 2024 tightening and conflicts with the "no extra email" guidance; treat as outdated. — [Boloo 2022: Reviews verzamelen, fix je e-mails](https://www.boloo.co/bibliotheek/reviews-verzamelen-op-bol-com-fix-je-e-mails)
- **[agency]** Lijstify has a 2026 article on what works and what does not, but the page returned HTTP 522 and could not be read. — [Lijstify: reviews verzamelen 2026](https://lijstify.com/blog/bol-com-reviews-verzamelen-2026) (not verified)

### Inferences
- The defensible 2026 playbook is: (1) let bol's own invitation run (the seller cannot control it); (2) include one neutral review sentence + link in the legally required invoice email; (3) an in-box insert/QR asking for "an honest review on bol" with no incentive; (4) never gate, filter ("only if happy") or reward reviews. The conflict between bol's page ("sellers cannot contact customers requesting reviews") and the invoice-mail carve-out means the seller should keep the request incidental to the invoice, not a separate campaign.
- Because seller rating is separate and product reviews do not affect the koopblok for a single-seller EAN, review effort should be justified on conversion and search ranking, not koopblok.

### Gaps
- I could not retrieve the exact bol text on inserts/QR cards; bol's productbeoordelingen page is silent on package inserts, and the "no extra contact" reading comes from Sellerly/Bolmate.
- No public data on what share of bol customers opt in to review invitations or bol's own invitation response rate.
- No documented sanction ladder for review-rule violations beyond removal of reviews and the general right to restrict selling rights.

## Key Question 4: How does bol rank FBR vs FBB/LVB offers, does "Select" change conversion, and what are the 2026 conditions to get Select or show "morgen in huis" as an FBR seller?

### Takeaway
bol states the koopblok goes to the "best total offer for the customer" using price incl. shipping, delivery promise/time, and kwaliteitsscore; bol says Verzenden via bol / Logistiek via bol strengthens the delivery promise and koopblok chance but gives no percentages. An FBR seller can show "vandaag voor 23:59 besteld, morgen in huis" by activating "Mijn Leverbelofte" (cut-off per weekday, working/delivery days matched to the carrier). The consumer-facing "Select" label is a bol membership delivery benefit (evening/Sunday/same-day, no shipping cost), attached to LVB and bol's own 1-day promise; the only partner-side Select mechanism found is "Select-deals" (kwaliteitsscore >= 70) and the 2025 "Altijd Select-kortingen" pilot for selected partners.

### Cited Findings
- **[bol rule]** Koopblok criteria per bol (quoted by Storbit): price incl. shipping, delivery time/promise, kwaliteitsscore; bol: "het beste totaalaanbod voor de klant, oftewel de verkoper met de grootste kans dat de klant tevreden is", not automatically the cheapest. — [Storbit: Koopblok winnen op bol 2026](https://storbit.nl/blog/koopblok-winnen-bol)
- **[bol rule]** "From June 24 [2026], the quality score has replaced the performance score completely. This means that the quality score counts for the buy box." "Hoe hoger je score, hoe groter je kans op de beste positie in het koopblok." — [Partnerplatform: Kwaliteitsscore](https://partnerplatform.bol.com/en/need-help/performance/quality-score/); [Partnerplatform NADP: kwaliteitsscore vervangt prestatiescore](https://partnerplatform.bol.com/nl/nadp/kwaliteitsscore-vervangt-prestatiescore-mei-2026)
- **[bol rule via agency]** "Verzend je via Verzenden via bol / Logistiek via bol, dan versterk je je leverbelofte: bol geeft zelf aan dat je met LVB meer kans maakt op het koopblok"; LVB articles typically show "vandaag besteld, morgen in huis" incl. evening and weekend delivery; no percentage disclosed. — [Storbit](https://storbit.nl/blog/koopblok-winnen-bol); [Boloo: Bol verzendkosten 2026](https://boloo.co/en/blog/bol-verzendkosten)
- **[bol rule]** Verzenden via bol Compleet advertises "voor 23:59 besteld, morgen in huis" as the ultimate promise; Standaard vs Compleet availability depends on your collection area; Compleet decision cut-offs referenced at 15:00/17:00; Friday-Sunday order delivery times differ per logistics service; promises can be temporarily adjusted in peak periods. — [Partnerplatform: Verzenden via bol Compleet](https://partnerplatform.bol.com/nl/cdp/verzenden-via-bol-com-compleet); [Partnerplatform: Leverbeloftes VVB](https://partnerplatform.bol.com/nl/idp/leverbeloftes-vvb); [Partnerplatform: logistieke diensten 2026](https://partnerplatform.bol.com/nl/cdp/lsvb-campagne-2026)
- **[bol rule]** 2026 VVB changes: from 13 July 2026 mail shipments via VVB go through PostNL "Brievenbuspakje+" with a next-day promise; tariffs adjusted 9 September 2026. — [Partnerplatform: nieuwe aanleverwijze PostNL briefpost](https://partnerplatform.bol.com/nl/nadp/nieuwe-aanleverwijze-postnl-briefpost)
- **[bol rule]** Own-shipping delivery times: changed per item (pencil icon) or in bulk, processed in ~15 minutes; "Mijn Leverbelofte" changes take ~3 hours; the page explicitly offers "vandaag voor 23.59 uur besteld, morgen in huis" as an option for sellers who can ship faster. — [Partnerplatform: Levertijd wijzigen via je verkoopaccount](https://partnerplatform.bol.com/nl/idp/levertijd-aanpassen-via-verkoopaccount)
- **[bol rule]** "Mijn Leverbelofte": sellers set the delivery times customers see; multiple profiles per assortment/logistics flow; cut-off time per weekday, working days and delivery days; linked to items or item groups; found under Settings > Leverbelofte > Activate; bol lists as benefits fewer strikes and complaints, better kwaliteitsscore, higher conversion, higher koopblok chance, more trust; the configuration "must match the agreements you have with your delivery service(s)". — [Partnerplatform: Stel Mijn Leverbelofte in](https://partnerplatform.bol.com/en/need-help/offer/set-up-my-delivery-promise/); [Vendiro guide: Mijn Leverbelofte](https://guide.vendiro.nl/article/301-bol-com-feature-mijn-leverbelofte)
- **[bol rule]** bol publishes downloadable tables of the latest delivery date per shown promise: 1-day (LVB and Select), 1-2 workdays, 2-3, 3-5, 4-8 and 1-8 workdays; Saturday delivery and Saturday processing can be switched on via PartnerService, which shortens weekend-spanning deadlines by one day. — [Partnerplatform: Uiterste leverdatum per getoonde leverbelofte](https://partnerplatform.bol.com/nl/idp/uiterste-leverdatum-per-getoonde-leverbelofte)
- **[bol rule]** Select (consumer side): members get standard, evening, same-day and Sunday delivery without extra shipping costs, plus Select-Deals discounts; availability of extra delivery options is shown per article; partner orders may arrive in multiple boxes. — [bol.com klantenservice: Select](https://www.bol.com/nl/nl/klantenservice/a/5692853414002688/select); [bol.com: Select voorwaarden](https://www.bol.com/nl/nl/tc/select-voorwaarden/)
- **[bol rule]** Kwaliteitsscore 70+ gives access to the Select-deals programme. — [Partnerplatform NADP: kwaliteitsscore vervangt prestatiescore](https://partnerplatform.bol.com/nl/nadp/kwaliteitsscore-vervangt-prestatiescore-mei-2026)
- **[agency]** "Altijd Select-kortingen" (June 2025): first time a selected group of partners could fund continuous Select-member discounts on their offers; "mogelijk conversieverhogend"; extra visibility for bestsellers; margin comes from the seller. — [Sellerly: Bol lanceert Altijd Select-kortingen](https://sellerly.nl/blog/bol-lanceert-altijd-select-kortingen-voor-geselecteerde-partners)
- **[agency]** Distilo (2026): "more than 7% late dispatch on a SKU impacts Buy Box within 1-2 weeks"; LVB/VVB fee increases January 2026; LVB slow-mover surcharge 40% after 270 days from March 2026. Labelled by Distilo as based on public seller cases, not internal bol knowledge. — [Distilo: koopblok terugwinnen 2026](https://www.distilo.nl/blog/koopblok-terugwinnen-2026)
- **[forum, older]** Higherlevel thread on how Select is compensated for partner logistics (not verified, older). — [Higherlevel forum](https://www.higherlevel.nl/forums/topic/59164-select-van-bolcom-hoe-werkt-dat-nu-als-vergoeding-voor-de-logistiek-partner/)

### Inferences
- For a single-seller private-label EAN there is no koopblok contest; the delivery promise matters for **conversion and search filters** ("morgen in huis" filter/sort), not for winning a buy box. Bline with eFreight same-day dispatch can legitimately configure Mijn Leverbelofte to "voor [cut-off] besteld, morgen in huis" on weekdays, provided the cut-off equals eFreight's actual cut-off and delivery days match the carrier (no Sunday/Monday promise unless the carrier delivers).
- Showing a promise the 3PL cannot keep converts into "Op tijd geleverd" misses, which now (from 27 July 2026) carry a 93% target / strike risk for own shipping; the safe design is a conservative cut-off and Saturday handling only if eFreight ships Saturdays.
- The partner-side "Select" label is not something an FBR seller can earn in 2026; the closest levers are LVB (gets 1-day + Select delivery options) or kwaliteitsscore >= 70 for Select-deals.

### Gaps
- bol does not publish the koopblok weighting or any conversion delta between 1-day and 1-2 day promises; no agency provided a bol-specific number.
- The "leverbeloftescore" (delivery promise score, min 60 for Groeibeloning) is referenced by bol but I found no page defining how it is calculated.
- The exact latest-delivery-date tables are downloads not readable via fetch; cut-off rules for the 1-2 workday promise in Belgium were not captured.
- I found no bol page stating conditions under which an FBR offer can carry the consumer "Select" label; the evidence indicates it is tied to LVB/bol logistics.

## Key Question 5: Kwaliteitsscore: how is it computed, what score is needed for Groeibeloning in Q4 2026, what does Groeibeloning pay and when?

### Takeaway
The kwaliteitsscore (0-100) is a weighted average of five service norms (artikelen op tijd, annuleringen, Track & Trace, retouren, klantvragen) over a rolling 22-week window; since 24 June 2026 it fully replaced the prestatiescore and counts for the koopblok. bol thresholds: 65 minimum, 69 + leverbeloftescore 60 + 2-year account for Groeibeloning (from 1 July 2026), 70+ for Select-deals, 75+ for strike leniency. Groeibeloning gives three personal quarterly revenue targets each unlocking a higher (undisclosed) percentage discount on variable commission, applied to new orders from the day after the target is hit until quarter end; eligibility is fixed ~3 weeks before each quarter.

### Cited Findings
- **[bol rule]** "The quality score is a weighted average of 5 service standards. We measure these over a period of 22 weeks." Scale 0-100. Minimum 65; 70 or higher grants extra benefits; 75+ combined with on-time in the 90-93% (own shipping) or 93-95% (VVB) band prevents strikes; Groeibeloning from 1 July 2026 requires 69 plus a delivery promise score of 60. Per-norm "target value" and "minimum value" exist; exceeding targets on some norms can compensate elsewhere. Weights are not disclosed. — [Partnerplatform: Kwaliteitsscore](https://partnerplatform.bol.com/en/need-help/performance/quality-score/)
- **[bol rule]** Replacement date Wednesday 24 June 2026; window extended from 6 to 22 weeks and norms from 3 to 5 (returns and customer questions added); score now shown per shipping method (eigen verzending, Verzenden via bol, Logistiek via bol); day-level force-majeure corrections (strikes, extreme weather). — [Partnerplatform NADP: kwaliteitsscore vervangt prestatiescore](https://partnerplatform.bol.com/nl/nadp/kwaliteitsscore-vervangt-prestatiescore-mei-2026); [Ecommerce News: Bol zet punt achter prestatiescore](https://www.ecommercenews.nl/bol-zet-punt-achter-prestatiescore/)
- **[bol rule]** Both scores were shown side by side from 1 October 2025 until the switch. — [Storbit](https://storbit.nl/blog/koopblok-winnen-bol); [Bolmate: bol kwaliteitsscore](https://bolmate.nl/bol-kwaliteitsscore/)
- **[bol rule]** "Artikelen op tijd" change effective 27 July 2026: split into "Op tijd overhandigd" (VVB, target 95%, no strike in 93-95% band if KS >= 75) and "Op tijd geleverd" (own shipping, target 93%, no strike in 90-93% band if KS >= 75). First strikes under the new rules computed 12 August 2026 for week-31 orders. Change affects strikes only, "no impact on quality score or buy box". Strikes expire after 22 weeks. A strike can be erased after 2 consecutive final weeks with: >= 20 measurable items, Op tijd geleverd >= 98.5% (VVB 100%), cancellations <= 0.5%, >= 90% customer questions answered within 24 hours; the strike week itself must have had >= 80% on time (own shipping) / 85% (VVB), >= 70% questions answered, <= 10% cancellations. — [Partnerplatform NADP: Servicenorm 'Artikelen op tijd' verandert](https://partnerplatform.bol.com/nl/nadp/servicenorm-artikelen-op-tijd-verandert)
- **[bol rule]** Strike process: strikes tracked over a rolling 22-week window (oldest week drops as a new "definitief berekende week" is added); 1-4 strikes = warning; 5 strikes = temporary shop closure and improvement plan within 3 weeks; 6 = final chance; 7+ = account closed for at least one year. — [Partnerplatform: Het strike proces](https://partnerplatform.bol.com/nl/hulp-nodig/prestaties/aangesproken-op-prestaties/)
- **[bol rule]** Cancellations: a strike when you cancel more than 2% of orders **and** there are three or more cancellations in a week. — [Partnerplatform (via search summary of strike/servicenorm pages)](https://partnerplatform.bol.com/nl/hulp-nodig/prestaties/aangesproken-op-prestaties/); [Rylee: belang van servicenormen](https://rylee.nl/nl/blog/het-belang-van-servicenormen-als-je-verkoopt-op-bol.com)
- **[bol rule]** Track & Trace norm: % of orders with a T&T code where required; minimum 90%, target 98%; mandatory for parcel/own shipping/bol labels/scheduled deliveries, recommended but optional for letterbox mail; must be added immediately on dispatch and cannot be changed afterwards. — [Partnerplatform: Track & Trace](https://partnerplatform.bol.com/nl/idp/track-and-trace)
- **[bol rule]** Klantvragen norm: percentage of customer questions over total orders, with a personal dynamic norm based on the categories sold; during GroeiStart the personal norm is multiplied by 1.125; a minimum of 3 customer questions applies before it bites; personal norm capped at 15%. — [Partnerplatform: Klantvragen](https://partnerplatform.bol.com/en/need-help/performance/service-standards-bol-com/customer-questions/)
- **[bol rule]** Retouren norm: percentage of articles returned; dynamic target per product group (downloadable table); bol tries to exclude unavoidable returns such as multi-size orders; "counts for the quality score but currently not for strikes"; improve via accurate descriptions, realistic pricing, return-reason reports. — [Partnerplatform: Retouren](https://partnerplatform.bol.com/nl/idp/retouren)
- **[bol rule]** Service standard list also includes phone availability, review score, response time and NPS after contact, but the kwaliteitsscore uses only the five above; scores visible on the "Prestaties" page of the seller account. — [Partnerplatform: Servicenormen](https://partnerplatform.bol.com/en/need-help/performance/service-standards-bol-com/)

### Inferences
- A score of ~69 is exactly at the Groeibeloning floor and 6 points below the 75 leniency band. Q4 2026 eligibility was determined around 10 September 2026 (three weeks before 1 October), so the week-37 cancellation and customer case (mid-September) fall after that cut but inside the 22-week window that determines Q1 2027 eligibility (cut ~10 December 2026) and every koopblok evaluation in Q4.
- With low weekly volumes, one cancellation can exceed 2% but only triggers a strike if there are >= 3 cancellations in that week; the bigger risk for a small seller is that a single event weighs heavily in a percentage-based norm for 22 weeks. Clean weeks (like week 38) dilute it slowly because the window is long.
- The "erase a strike" route (2 consecutive weeks with >= 20 measurable items, >= 98.5% on time, <= 0.5% cancellations, >= 90% questions answered within 24 h) is achievable in Q4 volume for a same-day 3PL seller if T&T is uploaded on every parcel and questions are answered within 24 h.

### Gaps

- The definition and calculation of the "leverbeloftescore" (>= 60) is not published on any page I could read.

- Whether the Q4 2026 cohort was admitted under "ruimere voorwaarden" (first-year relaxed conditions) versus the 69/60 rule is ambiguous in bol's wording: one page says the 69/60 thresholds apply "from 1 July 2026", another says relaxed conditions apply in the first year through 30 June 2027.

## Key Question 6: Returns policy effects, and a practical October checklist to lift conversion before Q4 peak

### Takeaway
bol's customer promise is 30 days to register a return and 14 days to ship it; own-shipping sellers must offer a free return route and process returns within days; return rate now feeds the kwaliteitsscore (not strikes). For October the highest-leverage, rule-compliant actions are: complete specs and 6+ images incl. 1200 px zoomable and scale shots, upload a product video, activate Mijn Leverbelofte with a truthful next-day cut-off, upload T&T on every parcel, answer every klantvraag within 24 h, add a neutral review line to the invoice mail plus an incentive-free insert, and audit the return-reason report.
