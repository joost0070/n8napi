# Bol.com Q4 2026: prijs, promoties en commerciële kalender voor een private-label leeskussen (69,99 / 79,99)

Research date: 27 September 2026. Seller context used for inferences: FBR/eigen verzending via 3PL (eFreight, next-day VVB), 248 units on hand + 248 arriving mid/late November, contribution ~16 EUR per pillow at 69,99 and ~23 EUR at 79,99 before ads, storage ~4,90 EUR/day. All monetary examples built on these inputs are labelled ESTIMATE.

Important caveat up front: bol's exact Q4 2026 campaign dates, deal-submission deadlines and the per-category commission table are only exposed inside the partner's verkoopaccount (campaign calendar, "Promoties", commission downloads). The public partnerplatform pages describe the mechanics but not the dates. Where I could not find a public 2026 date I say so and give the 2025/2024 pattern, labelled.

## Key question 1: Exact 2026 dates (Black Friday, Cyber Monday, Sinterklaas, bol's Black Friday window, kerst deadlines, when to submit deals)

### Takeaway
Black Friday 2026 is Friday 27 November and Cyber Monday is Monday 30 November; Sinterklaas (pakjesavond) 5 December 2026 falls on a Saturday and Christmas Day on a Friday. Bol publishes its Q4 campaign calendar and deal windows only inside the seller account ("at least 9 weeks in advance") and screens partners for campaigns "approximately 8 weeks before" go-live, which for a 27 Nov Black Friday means the selection/submission window is roughly late September to early October 2026, i.e. now.

Also stated as 27 November 2026 in [bol partnerplatform search context](https://partnerplatform.bol.com/en/need-help/offer/participating-in-promotions-in-bol-campaigns/). ### Inferences
- Calendar arithmetic (derived from the confirmed 27 Nov date): Sinterklaas/pakjesavond Saturday 5 Dec 2026 means the last realistic next-day delivery slot for Sinterklaas gifts is orders placed Thursday 3 Dec (delivered Fri 4 Dec) or, if the 3PL/carrier delivers on Saturday, orders on Fri 4 Dec. Christmas Day Friday 25 Dec 2026: Thursday 24 Dec is a normal working day, so a next-day VVB promise implies last orders on Wed 23 Dec for Thu 24 Dec delivery — ESTIMATE; confirm with eFreight's Q4 carrier cut-offs.

The seller should open "Promoties" in the verkoopaccount this week and check the Q4 campaign calendar rather than wait for public announcements.
- The second stock batch "arriving mid/late November" lands inside the historical 19 Nov–3 Dec peak; if it arrives after ~20 November the first 248 units must cover Black Friday week. Given Boloo's claim that mid-December matches the Black Friday weekend, the second batch is still well-timed for the 13–23 December Christmas window.

## Key question 2: How bol weekdeals / dagdeals / Black Friday deals are selected, expected discount depth, uplift during deals and rank effect afterwards

### Takeaway
Bol selects campaign deals centrally (sales, views, review score, quality requirements) about 8 weeks before go-live and lets partners propose items; some deal types require a set discount percentage while others accept a "scherp geprijsd" offer. Bol's own guidance claims partner-run promotions lift sales 20–40% and leave "permanently higher positions in list pages and more reviews"; independent quantitative evidence on post-deal rank is thin.

### Cited Findings
- Two routes: (1) bol marketing campaigns (bol 7-daagse, Sale/Solden, Sinterklaas, Black Friday, plus the dagdeal) and (2) own promotions where the partner picks timing and assortment. "Some of these promotions require a certain discount percentage, but there are also options to participate with a 'sharply priced' offer — no discount is required, but the price must be good relative to the market" — [Partnerplatform: Promotiemogelijkheden](https://partnerplatform.bol.com/nl/hulp-nodig/aanbod/promotiemogenlijkheden/).
- Own-promotion types available in the seller account: volume discounts, "X+Y free", Select deals, own promotions with chosen duration; bol says these can be amplified with Sponsored Products — [Partnerplatform: Promotiemogelijkheden (idp)](https://partnerplatform.bol.com/nl/idp/promotiemogelijkheden).
- Selection: "based on, among other things, your sales, views, and review scores"; "Participating in promotions costs nothing extra, but there are conditions attached"; you must have offers on the selected campaign items — [Partnerplatform: Participating in promotions in bol campaigns](https://partnerplatform.bol.com/en/need-help/offer/participating-in-promotions-in-bol-campaigns/).
- Uplift claim: with own promotions "sales can increase by 20% to 40%"; after campaigns "partners see permanently higher positions in list pages and more reviews" — [Partnerplatform: Promotions via bol](https://partnerplatform.bol.com/en/services/promotions-via-bol/).
- Bol states "customers are very sensitive to high discounts" and "items with a fast delivery time show a higher conversion" — [Partnerplatform: Promotiemogelijkheden (idp)](https://partnerplatform.bol.com/nl/idp/promotiemogelijkheden).
- Many promotion visitors "do not yet have a clear purchase intent" and the most effective way to convert them is a sharp price — [Partnerplatform (search context)](https://partnerplatform.bol.com/nl/idp/promotiemogelijkheden).
- Promotion eligibility on price stars: items with 1+ price stars are eligible for bol promotions subject to other quality criteria (sales history, review scores) — [Partnerplatform: Price stars](https://partnerplatform.bol.com/en/idp/price-stars).
- Select-deals require a kwaliteitsscore of 70+ — [Partnerplatform: kwaliteitsscore vervangt prestatiescore](https://partnerplatform.bol.com/nl/nadp/kwaliteitsscore-vervangt-prestatiescore-mei-2026).
- "Altijd Select-kortingen" (since mid-2025) let a selected group of partners run ongoing discounts for Select members on products bol designates as popular; "the discount comes from your margin" — [Sellerly](https://sellerly.nl/blog/bol-lanceert-altijd-select-kortingen-voor-geselecteerde-partners).
- Consumer-side deal depth at bol Black Friday: headline "up to 50%+" / "up to 70%" with category ranges 30–50% — [blackfridayshoppen.nl](https://www.blackfridayshoppen.nl/winkels/bol-com/), [blackfridaydeals.nu](https://www.blackfridaydeals.nu/bol-com/). Bol's own 2024 examples: 20% off an airfryer, 30% off a video doorbell — [FTM](https://www.ftm.nl/artikelen/interview-bolcom-b-corp-black-friday).
- Halo claim from an agency: after the bol 7-daagse "you'll often see a halo effect of better visibility and stronger search rankings long after the deals end" — [10xcrew](https://10xcrew.com/bol-7-daagse-strategy-timing-and-how-to-win-on-bol/) (page body could not be fetched; claim comes from search snippet, treat as agency opinion).
- Peak-season uplift claim (agency, unverified): sales/conversion "300 tot wel 700%" higher in peak months — [Boloo piekmaanden](https://www.boloo.co/bibliotheek/de-bol-com-piekmaanden-een-guide-voor-bol-com-verkopers).
- Reviews compound: products with more than 10 reviews have on average a 30% higher conversion rate than products without reviews (agency claim) — [Boloo reviews](https://boloo.co/blog/bol-com-review).
- Commission during promotions is calculated on the (discounted) selling price — [Partnerplatform: De commissie](https://partnerplatform.bol.com/nl/hulp-nodig/financien/de-commissie/).

### Inferences
- Bol does not publish a fixed minimum discount for Black Friday/weekdeals publicly; the "scherp geprijsd" route exists, but the campaign-side consumer messaging (20–50%) and bol's own statement that customers are "very sensitive to high discounts" suggest a private-label pillow at 69,99 will need a visible, meaningful percentage (ESTIMATE: 15–25%) to be picked for a Black Friday weekdeal, whereas a dagdeal or own promotion can be done at 10–15%.
- The strongest documented post-deal benefits are the ones bol itself names: more reviews and higher list-page positions. For a young private-label listing with few reviews, the review accumulation from a deal week is probably worth more than the deal-week margin, because the 30%-conversion-lift-above-10-reviews effect persists into the December Christmas surge.
- Since commission is charged on the discounted price, roughly 13% (see Q6) of every euro of discount is "paid" by bol, softening the margin hit slightly.

### Gaps
- No independent, quantitative data on rank position before vs after a bol deal (only bol's qualitative "permanently higher positions" and agency "halo" claims).
- No public bol document stating minimum discount % per deal type (dagdeal/weekdeal/Black Friday) or minimum stock per deal; these appear only in the Promoties tool when proposing an item.

## Key question 3: Prijssterren thresholds and the "onzichtbaarheidsgrens"

### Takeaway
Bol scores each item 1–5 price stars against a "relevant market price" built from bol history, external channels and RRP; 1 star means the offer is effectively not shown, 3+ stars unlocks bol's Google-Ads (SEA) spend, 4 stars is "generally fine". The exact euro thresholds ("grensniveaus") are not public; repricers learn them empirically, and Vendiro caps at 2 stars by default to stay online.

### Cited Findings
- Definition: "Price stars indicate how sharp the price of an item is compared with other sellers"; bol compares "not only on bol but also elsewhere"; "If you have 4 price stars you're generally doing well"; visible in the artikelenoverzicht and via API; some items get no star for lack of comparison data — [Partnerplatform: Prijssterren (NL)](https://partnerplatform.bol.com/nl/idp/prijssterren).
- Benefits: higher stars give "more visitors to your webshop, a higher ranking in search results, and more clicks"; 3+ stars qualifies for SEA budget (varies by category); 1+ stars needed for bol promotions — [Partnerplatform: Price stars (EN)](https://partnerplatform.bol.com/en/idp/price-stars).
- Star meanings per Rylee: 1 star = "poorly priced with a significant difference to the market price" and such products "are not displayed in search results"; 5 stars = sharpest price vs market. Relevant Market Price (RMP) uses competitor prices on and off bol, recommended retail prices, historical bol pricing and "the most frequently used price" — [Rylee: prijssterren](https://rylee.nl/nl/blog/how-do-you-choose-the-right-price-and-what-influence-do-price-stars-have-on-this).
- Channable: 1 star = "too expensive, and it won't be shown on Bol.com"; 5 stars = "very competitive and will lead to optimal conversion"; bol also weighs delivery times, reviews, customer-satisfaction score — [Channable blog](https://www.channable.com/blog/bol-marketplace-price-stars).
- Boundary mechanics: "You get 1 star when the product price is higher than boundary level 2"; price-star boundaries "are currently available for 60% of the bol assortment"; Vendiro's repricer defaults the max price to price star 2 — [Vendiro guide (search context)](https://guide.vendiro.nl/article/505-bol-com-prijssterren).
- Repricer behaviour: when bol rejects a price as non-market-conforming and takes the listing offline, the repricer "learns where that boundary lies per article and adjusts just below it" — [Stockitup repricer (search context)](https://stockitup.nl/oplossingen/repricer/).
- Bol runs a webinar "Prijsstrategie als groeiversneller" covering why "small differences" in price on the product page have impact, dynamic pricing, and the "Nu in prijs verlaagd" label that can appear without an active promotion — [Partnerplatform webinar page](https://partnerplatform.bol.com/nl/ledp/webinar-prijsstrategie-als-groeiversneller).
- Pricing tactic (agency): raise bestseller prices before late November, then lower them to trigger the "prijs verlaagd" label — [Boloo piekmaanden](https://www.boloo.co/bibliotheek/de-bol-com-piekmaanden-een-guide-voor-bol-com-verkopers). Note: EU price-indication rules (Omnibus) require reference to the lowest price in the prior 30 days for discount claims; bol computes the label itself, so this tactic is risky and not endorsed here.

### Inferences
- For a private-label EAN with no other sellers, the RMP is driven by bol's own price history for that EAN and by "similar" market/RRP data. A seller who holds 69,99 consistently effectively sets their own reference price; a sustained deep discount risks resetting the RMP downward so that the later return to 69,99 scores fewer stars. Time-boxed promotions (dagdeal, one weekdeal) are less likely to move the reference than weeks of a lower "normal" price — ESTIMATE/inference, not documented by bol.
- Because SEA (bol-funded Google Shopping traffic) needs 3+ stars, keeping the white pillow at 3–4 stars during Q4 is worth free external traffic; check stars weekly in the artikelenoverzicht before each campaign.

### Gaps
- Bol does not publish the numeric thresholds (what % above RMP yields 2 vs 1 star); no source gives them. The Channable helpcenter article on boundary levels returned 404.
- No documented interaction between prijssterren and the koopblok for single-seller private label (irrelevant if there is no competing offer on the EAN, but relevant if resellers appear).

## Key question 4: Break-even pricing vs holding margin; hold price and buy ads, or discount for volume and rank (price elasticity for gift/home-comfort in Q4)

### Takeaway
No bol seller case study with hard elasticity numbers for home-comfort/gift products was found. The available evidence: bol claims 20–40% uplift from promotions and lasting rank/review gains; Q4 CPCs on bol run 20–40% above baseline (Wonen & Slapen baseline 0,30–1,00 EUR); and NL Black Friday demand has spread across "Black November" with 41% of tech sold at 15%+ discount. Arithmetic on the seller's own contribution shows a 10% discount on the 69,99 pillow needs ~1.9x volume to break even, whereas on the 79,99 bundle it needs ~1.5x — so any discounting is cheaper on the bundle.

### Cited Findings
- Bol Sponsored Products CPC ranges by category (agency figures): Wonen & Slapen 0,30–1,00 EUR; peak periods (Black Friday, Sinterklaas, kerst) "CPC's 20-40% hoger"; ACoS targets 20–30% (growth), 10–15% (profitable), <8% (max profit); advice to raise ad budget 2–3 weeks before the peak — [Boloo: adverteren op bol.com](https://boloo.co/blog/adverteren-op-bol-com).
- Some sources claim CPCs in popular categories can be 2–3x normal in Nov–Dec — [WinkelBoekje (search context)](https://winkelboekje.nl/blog/bol-com-reclame-kosten-roi/).
- NL Black Friday 2025: 41% of tech products sold with 15%+ discount; 70% of "Black November" shoppers also bought in the previous year's event; spending spread over all of November — [NIQ](https://nielseniq.com/global/nl/news-center/2025/black-friday-is-uit-black-november-is-in/).
- Bol: own promotions lift sales 20–40% and leave "permanently higher positions in list pages and more reviews" — [Partnerplatform: Promotions via bol](https://partnerplatform.bol.com/en/services/promotions-via-bol/).
- Bol: "customers are very sensitive to high discounts"; fast delivery time raises conversion — [Partnerplatform: Promotiemogelijkheden (idp)](https://partnerplatform.bol.com/nl/idp/promotiemogelijkheden).
- December gift purchases generate fewer returns than typical orders; January sales drop sharply (agency observation) — [Boloo piekmaanden](https://www.boloo.co/bibliotheek/de-bol-com-piekmaanden-een-guide-voor-bol-com-verkopers).
- Agency pricing advice: dynamic pricing with higher prices during peak demand and lower prices off-peak; repricers claimed to raise buy-box win chances "by 80%" (unverified marketing claim) — [Boloo: prijzen veranderen (search context)](https://boloo.co/blog/prijzen-bol-com-veranderen).
- Commission is charged on the discounted selling price, so bol shares the discount pro rata — [Partnerplatform: De commissie](https://partnerplatform.bol.com/nl/hulp-nodig/financien/de-commissie/).
- Groeibeloning discounts on variable commission apply from the day after a quarterly revenue target is hit until period end — [Partnerplatform: Groeibeloning](https://partnerplatform.bol.com/nl/hulp-nodig/prestaties/groeibeloning/) (relevant because Q4 volume can unlock lower commission on all subsequent orders, see Q6).

### Inferences (all ESTIMATES built on the seller's stated contributions and an assumed 13% variable commission; the actual kussens rate must be read from the seller account)
- Break-even volume multiplier for a discount (contribution saved on commission ≈ 13% of the discount):
  - White pillow 69,99, contribution 16: −10% (−7,00) → contribution ≈ 16 − 7,00 + 0,91 = 9,9 → need ~1.6x units; −15% (−10,50) → ≈ 6,9 → ~2.3x; −20% (−14,00) → ≈ 3,8 → ~4.2x.
  - Bundle 79,99, contribution 23: −10% (−8,00) → ≈ 16,0 → ~1.4x; −15% (−12,00) → ≈ 12,6 → ~1.8x; −20% (−16,00) → ≈ 9,1 → ~2.5x.
  - Reading: the bundle can absorb a 10–15% headline discount and still keep >50% of its contribution; the white pillow cannot go beyond ~10% without needing more than a doubling of volume.
- Ads instead of discount: at Q4 CPC of ~0,40–1,40 EUR (0,30–1,00 +20–40%) and an assumed 4–6% conversion (no bol benchmark found), cost per ad-driven order is roughly 7–35 EUR, i.e. an ACoS of 10–50% at 69,99. At the "profitable" 10–15% ACoS target the ad cost per order (7–10,50 EUR) is comparable to a 10–15% discount, but it is paid only on incremental ad-driven orders, not on every organic order. That is the core argument for "hold price + ads" for the white pillow, and "modest discount on the bundle + ads" for the gift positioning.
- Volume the stock can support: 496 units total across Q4. A 20% Black Friday weekdeal on the 69,99 pillow would need ~4x baseline volume to pay for itself in contribution; given only 248 units on hand before the second batch, a deep discount risks selling out before the historically equally strong 13–23 December Christmas window (per Boloo/Rylee), which is lower-return-rate, higher-price gift demand.
- Storage at 4,90 EUR/day is ~150 EUR/month, ≈0,30 EUR per unit per month across 496 units, so carrying stock into January is cheap; the real cost of holding price is unsold units in a demand trough, not storage. This argues against discounting merely to clear inventory before Christmas.
- A middle path consistent with bol's rules: keep 69,99 as the reference price through October (build price-history and reviews), run one time-boxed bol campaign slot on the bundle at −10% to −15% (Sinterklaas or Black Friday week), and spend ads at 10–15% ACoS on the white pillow. This maximises reviews and list-page rank going into 13–23 December when demand is highest and returns lowest.

### Gaps
- No bol seller case study with quantified price elasticity or "break-even to gain rank" numbers for home-comfort or gift products was found; agency content is qualitative.
- No bol-specific conversion-rate benchmark for Wonen/kussens to turn CPC into cost per order; the 4–6% used above is an assumption.
- No published bol data on ad CPC by week in Q4 2025.

## Key question 5: Bundle and cover strategies; gift positioning (Sinterklaas, kerst); seasonality of "leeskussen" demand NL/BE

### Takeaway
Bol allows partner-created bundles under one EAN (max 4 EANs, price not above the sum of parts, items also sold separately), and offers native "X+Y gratis" and volume-discount promotions. Bol's own Q4 marketing frames Sinterklaas and Kerst around "a gift that matters"/wish lists. I found no quantitative search-volume data for "leeskussen" (Google Trends could not be fetched); the only seasonal anchors are generic (Sinterklaas searches peak in the last weeks of November, Christmas 15–28 December).

### Cited Findings
- Bundle rules: a bundle is "a combination of different items sold under one EAN"; max 4 different EANs; all items GS1-registered including the bundle EAN; "individual items must be offered separately"; "the price of a bundle may not be higher than the individual items combined"; content attributes follow the primary item; all EANs and descriptions must appear in the listing; unbranded-only bundles can be registered by the partner and marketed as unbranded; non-compliant bundles risk removal and policy points — [Partnerplatform: Bundels](https://partnerplatform.bol.com/en/need-help/offer/bundle-deals/).
- Native promotion formats partners can create themselves: volume discounts, "X+Y free", Select deals, own promotions — [Partnerplatform: Promotiemogelijkheden (idp)](https://partnerplatform.bol.com/nl/idp/promotiemogelijkheden).
- Bol's Sinterklaas campaigns 2025: "Sinterklaas and Father Christmas together... For a gift that matters" and a wish-list-driven campaign — [over.bol.com](https://over.bol.com/en/news/bol-brings-sinterklaas-and-father-christmas-together-in-sinterklaas-campaign-for-a-gift-that-matters/), [over.bol.com wish lists](https://over.bol.com/en/news/bol-s-new-sinterklaas-campaign-brings-wish-lists-to-life/); bol's holiday message "gifts that truly matter" — [over.bol.com](https://over.bol.com/en/news/bol-celebrates-the-holidays-with-gifts-that-truly-matter/).
- Bol has historically reported Christmas overtaking Sinterklaas in order volume ("Kerstmis haalt Sinterklaas in bij bol.com: alle records verbroken", older press release, year not verified) — [pers.bol.com](https://pers.bol.com/nl/nieuws/kerstmis-haalt-sinterklaas-in-bij-bol-com-alle-records-verbroken/).
- Gift framing of the product category by a gift-idea site: leeskussen met nekrol positioned as the "ultimate comfort accessory for every book lover", also for students — [Cadeaulab](https://www.cadeaulab.nl/cadeau/leeskussen-met-nekrol/).
- Generic NL seasonality (Google Trends index, from an SEO blog): "Black Friday" peaks in the last week of November (index 61), "Sinterklaas" peaks in the final weeks of November (78), "Kerst" hits 100 in the weeks 15–28 December — [Keurigonline: Google zoektrends 2025 NL](https://www.keurigonline.nl/blog/google-zoek-trends-2025-in-nederland). "Sinterklaas cadeau" search volume peaks in November and is dead in February — [SAM Online Marketing (search context)](https://www.samonlinemarketing.nl/begrippenlijst/algemeen/wat-is-zoekvolume/).
- Bol's top-5 holiday categories in the 2022 partner guidance were fragrances, body care, facial care, washing machines, coffee makers (home comfort not in top 5) — [Rylee](https://rylee.nl/nl/blog/hoe-bereid-je-jouw-bol-store-voor-op-de-feestdagen).
- December gift orders have lower return rates — [Boloo piekmaanden](https://www.boloo.co/bibliotheek/de-bol-com-piekmaanden-een-guide-voor-bol-com-verkopers).

### Inferences
- The 79,99 pillow+cover bundle is compliant as long as the cover is also listed separately with its own EAN and 79,99 ≤ pillow price + cover price. Because bol requires the parts to be sold separately, the seller should also list the colour cover alone (ESTIMATE 12,99–19,99) so that the bundle shows an implied saving and the cover becomes an accessory attach for earlier pillow buyers.
- "X+Y gratis" (e.g., pillow + free cover) is a native bol promotion type and would display as a gift-worthy offer without lowering the pillow's reference price for prijssterren — useful for Sinterklaas positioning while protecting the 69,99 anchor. Not documented whether X+Y promotions are eligible for campaign slots.
- Demand shape: the general NL pattern (late-November Sinterklaas/Black Friday peak, second Christmas peak 13–28 December) plus Boloo's claim that mid-December equals Black Friday weekend implies October is a build-up month for reviews and rank, not the month to discount.

### Gaps
- Google Trends for "leeskussen" (NL), "coussin de lecture" (BE) and "bookseat" could not be retrieved (no fetchable endpoint); no keyword-tool page with monthly volumes was found. Seasonality for this specific product is therefore unquantified; the October-vs-December multiple asked for cannot be given from sources.
- No bol data on attach rates of accessories or bundle performance vs single items.

## Key question 6: Commission and fee changes on bol for Q4 2026 (Wonen, Groeibeloning, Select)

### Takeaway
Bol changed commission rates on 9 September 2026 (tables for "until" and "from" that date are downloadable in the seller account); public trackers list woondecoratie at 13% (up from 12%) and meubels at 12%, but the exact line for kussens/woontextiel must be read from the account. Groeibeloning (live 1 July 2026) gives quarterly, tiered discounts on variable commission for accounts ≥2 years old with kwaliteitsscore ≥69 and bezorgbelofte-score ≥60; tier percentages are not published (third parties cite "up to 10–15%").

### Cited Findings
- Commission = fixed amount per item + variable % of selling price (incl. VAT basis, rates ex VAT), by category; new rates effective 9 September 2026 with separate downloadable tables for before/after; commission is calculated on the discounted price during promotions; example: Kitchen Merchandise variable 5–12,5%, fixed 0–0,85 EUR — [Partnerplatform: De commissie](https://partnerplatform.bol.com/nl/hulp-nodig/financien/de-commissie/), [Partnerplatform: Commission rates](https://partnerplatform.bol.com/en/need-help/finance/commission/commission-rates/).
- Third-party 2026 tables: woondecoratie 13% (up from 12%), meubels 12%, tuin 12%, keuken 12%, verlichting 11%, huishoudelijke apparaten 9%; kleding/schoenen 14%→15%, gezondheid 11%→10%, fietsen 10%→9%; fixed contributions cited as 1,96 (brievenbus) / 2,95 (klein) / 4,25 (groot) / 5,95 (extra groot) — [Winkelfactuur commissie 2026](https://winkelfactuur.nl/nl/blog/bol-com-commissie-tarieven-2026/). Generic fixed-fee bands: ~0,20 EUR (0–10), ~0,40 (10–20), often 2,99 (20+) — [MarktA](https://markta.nl/blog/bol-commissie-tarieven-2026/). Variable range across all categories 4,1%–20,7% — [Partnerplatform search context](https://partnerplatform.bol.com/nl/hulp-nodig/financien/de-commissie/). NOTE: these third-party tables are not the official 9-Sept-2026 file and conflict on fixed fees; treat as indicative only.
- Groeibeloning conditions from 1 July 2026: kwaliteitsscore ≥69, bezorgbelofte-score ≥60, account ≥2 years, ≥1 sale per year in the last 2 years; relaxed first-year conditions, stricter from 1 July 2027; quarterly periods; personal revenue targets from prior-year same-period revenue, recent performance, markets/categories, business size; each achieved target unlocks a progressively higher discount on variable commission from the next day until period end; a maximum revenue cap exists; per sales account; fraud = exclusion — [Partnerplatform: Groeibeloning](https://partnerplatform.bol.com/nl/hulp-nodig/prestaties/groeibeloning/).
- Three personal targets per quarter with increasing discount per target — [Partnerplatform search context](https://partnerplatform.bol.com/nl/hulp-nodig/prestaties/groeibeloning/); launch 1 July 2026, notifications from late March 2026 — [Twinkle](https://twinklemagazine.nl/2026/03/bol-start-nieuw-beloningsprogramma-voor-verkooppartners-die-goed-presteren/index.xml), [over.bol.com](https://over.bol.com/en/news/bol-introduces-new-rewards-programme/).
- Third-party sizing of the benefit: "up to 10–15% discount on the commission percentage" (not official) — [Winkelfactuur](https://winkelfactuur.nl/nl/blog/bol-com-commissie-tarieven-2026/). Dutchcowboys' critique: commissions "dalen stapsgewijs per kwartaal" only for qualifying sellers; long term, non-qualifying sellers pay relatively more — [Dutchcowboys](https://www.dutchcowboys.nl/online/bolcom-geeft-korting-op-commissie-aan-topverkopers-maar-er-zit-een-addertje-onder-het-gras).
- Kwaliteitsscore replaced the prestatiescore in the koopblok on 24 June 2026; measured over 22 weeks (was 6) on 5 service standards (was 3, now incl. returns and customer questions), with insight per shipping method (eigen verzending / Verzenden via bol / LVB); thresholds: 70+ Select-deals, 75+ strike coulance, 69+ Groeibeloning (year 1) — [Partnerplatform: kwaliteitsscore](https://partnerplatform.bol.com/nl/nadp/kwaliteitsscore-vervangt-prestatiescore-mei-2026).
- Select for partners: "Altijd Select-kortingen" (selected partners, bol-designated popular products, discount from seller margin, auto-activated when conditions are met) — [Sellerly](https://sellerly.nl/blog/bol-lanceert-altijd-select-kortingen-voor-geselecteerde-partners).
- Separate from seller commission: bol's affiliate programme moved to a new commission model (minimum 4% up to 8%, calculated ex VAT) with changes from early October 2026 — [affiliate.bol.com](https://affiliate.bol.com/nl/nieuws/nieuwe-commissiemodel/). Not a seller fee, but relevant if the seller uses affiliates.
- Platform scale: >50,000 active partners; trading volume +8.4% to 6.3 bn EUR — [RetailDetail (search context)](https://www.retaildetail.nl/news/algemeen/verkooppartners-stuwen-de-omzet-van-bol/).

### Inferences
- If the leeskussen is classified under a Wonen sub-category at ~12–13% variable plus a ~2,99–4,25 fixed fee (ESTIMATE from third-party tables), commission at 69,99 is roughly 11,4–13,3 EUR and at 79,99 roughly 12,6–14,6 EUR; the seller should verify the exact kussens/woontextiel line in the 9-Sept-2026 download because the 16/23 EUR contribution figures depend on it.
- Groeibeloning matters for Q4 only if the account is ≥2 years old with ≥1 sale in each of the last two years; if the account is younger it does not qualify in 2026 regardless of kwaliteitsscore. If it does qualify, the Q4 target is based on Q4 2025 revenue, so a seller that was small in Q4 2025 has a low bar and Q4 volume can unlock lower commission on all orders for the rest of the quarter — a real, if unquantified, argument for front-loading volume in October/November.
- The kwaliteitsscore's 22-week window means Q4 service slips (late shipments from the 3PL in peak) depress the score into spring 2027; protecting the next-day VVB promise during 19 Nov–23 Dec is worth more than an extra deal slot.

### Gaps
- The official commission percentage and fixed fee for the kussens/woontextiel category from 9 September 2026 is only in the downloadable table inside the seller account; not retrievable publicly.
- Groeibeloning tier percentages and the revenue cap are not published anywhere public.
- No public source on whether bol changed the fixed "verzendbijdrage"/fixed fee for Q4 2026 specifically.
