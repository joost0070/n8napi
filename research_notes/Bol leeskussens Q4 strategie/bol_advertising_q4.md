# bol.com advertising as a Q4 2026 growth lever (Sponsored Products, Branded Shelves, off-platform)

Research date: 2026-09-27. Scope: bol.com NL/BE. Sources are labelled by date; anything before 2025 is marked "OLDER". Amazon-derived material is explicitly labelled "AMAZON, not bol".

Seller context used for the inferences below: one AUTO Sponsored Products campaign per country (NL €10/day, BE €6/day) at target ACoS 15%; Branded Shelves €2/day; September conversion ~2% of ad clicks; contribution per pillow before ads ≈ €16 at €69,99 and ≈ €23 at €79,99; stock 248 now + 248 mid/late November.

---

## Key question 1: Is there evidence that ad-driven sales lift organic ranking on bol, and how large/lasting is it?

### Takeaway
Every bol-focused source (bol's own help pages, MarktMentor, Boloo, Bolmate) states that sales and clicks generated by Sponsored Products feed the same signals the organic algorithm uses (clicks, sales, conversion), and the one published bol case study shows a category-rank move from ~240 to ~15 during a 4-week campaign. Nobody publishes a controlled measurement of how large the lift is or how long it lasts after ads stop; the best available statement is that bol's ranking is a weighted moving average over roughly 28–90 days with the last 7 days weighted most heavily, so the effect decays over weeks rather than days.

### Cited Findings
- bol's organic category position is "primarily determined by two factors: clicks and sales", recent clicks/sales weigh heavier than older ones, described as a weighted average "over the past 28 to 90 days"; products can rise faster through "momentum" (many units sold in a short period) — [Rylee, Bol ranking optimaliseren 2026](https://rylee.nl/nl/blog/Bol-ranking-optimaliseren); [BrandMonks, Bol ranking factoren](https://brandmonks.nl/kennisbank/bol-ranking-factoren/); [Illustrify, Bol algoritme uitgelegd](https://illustrify.nl/inzichten/verkopen-op-bol-com/bol-algoritme/)
- Seller reliability signals (delivery reliability, reviews, return rate, delivery time) also feed ranking — [Rylee, Bol ranking optimaliseren 2026](https://rylee.nl/nl/blog/Bol-ranking-optimaliseren)
- OLDER (Nov 2023) MarktMentor case study: a 4-week Sponsored Products campaign moved a garden product from category position 240 to position 15; 220 sales from 2,951 clicks (7.45% conversion), €7,469 revenue on €2,685.83 spend (ACoS 35.96%, target ≈ margin 35%). The organic rank is "a weighted moving average", performance "from the last seven days" is emphasized, it "took several days before the lower pre-campaign performance was compensated", and growth gets harder near the top 25 — [MarktMentor 4/4](https://www.marktmentor.nl/blog/verbeteren-vindbaarheid-bol-rankings); [MarktMentor 3/4](https://www.marktmentor.nl/blog/verbeteren-vindbaarheid-bol-sponsored-products)
- Same case, by placement: category pages 7.86% conv / ACoS 26.05%; product pages 7.78% conv / ACoS 36.58%; search keywords 7.1% conv / ACoS 39.89%. Recommendation: run at least 4 weeks, set target ACoS = margin, judge keywords only after 50+ clicks — [MarktMentor 3/4](https://www.marktmentor.nl/blog/verbeteren-vindbaarheid-bol-sponsored-products)
- OLDER (Nov 2023) MarktMentor states performance "from up to four weeks prior" still influences current organic visibility — [MarktMentor 3/4](https://www.marktmentor.nl/blog/verbeteren-vindbaarheid-bol-sponsored-products)
- Bolmate (22 Dec 2025): "Extra verkopen en hogere conversie via Sponsored Products tellen mee in het organische algoritme" — described as a flywheel; also warns "Sponsored Products versterkt alleen wat er al staat" (ads only amplify existing content quality) — [Bolmate, Onmisbaar of geldverspilling?](https://bolmate.nl/bol-sponsored-products/)
- Boloo (May 2026): sponsored sales volumes help improve organic rankings ("positive feedback loop"); products that rank both organically and as sponsored get more buyer trust; advises monitoring organic position changes alongside ad campaigns — [Boloo, Sponsored Products 2026](https://boloo.co/blog/sponsored-products)
- Boloo (Apr 2026): for a new product launch, "begin with a higher ACoS (20-30%) to build volume quickly; the extra sales improve organic ranking, generate reviews and increase conversion", with the goal of becoming less dependent on paid placements — [Boloo, Adverteren op bol.com](https://boloo.co/blog/adverteren-op-bol-com)
- Boloo claims "78% of all clicks originate from the first page of search results" and "60% from the top 10" (no methodology given) — [Boloo, Adverteren op bol.com](https://boloo.co/blog/adverteren-op-bol-com)
- bol's SP auction score itself includes conversion history: "Veilingsscore = Relevantie × Verwachte CTR × Conversiepercentage × Biedprijs"; conversions counted within 14 days post-click, including direct, secondary (other products in the campaign) and tertiary (other seller products in the category) — OLDER (Nov 2023) [MarktMentor, Het algoritme achter bol Sponsored Products](https://www.marktmentor.nl/blog/bol-sponsored-products-algoritme)
- Reviews: products with 20+ reviews averaging 4+ stars sell approximately "2-3x" more than those with 0–5 reviews at equal price and buy-box position; 10–20% revenue lift after crossing 20 reviews; but "price and delivery time outweigh review count in bol's algorithm; a 5-review/4.5-star product often beats 50-review/4.0-star" (community field data, no independent verification) — [BolKit review benchmarks 2026, May 2026](https://bolkit.nl/blog/bol-com-review-conversie-benchmark-2026)

### Inferences
- The mechanism is documented (ad sales = organic sales in bol's signals), but the magnitude is only illustrated by one OLDER case in a low-competition category at ~7% conversion. At the seller's ~2% conversion, the same click volume buys roughly a quarter of the ranking signal, so a "buy rank" strategy will need proportionally more spend per rank step.
- Because the ranking window is a 28–90-day weighted average with 7-day emphasis, a burst of ad-driven sales in early-mid November should still be contributing to organic position through Sinterklaas (5 Dec) and Christmas, but it decays; the sources imply the rank must be "maintained" with continued sales rather than being permanently earned.
- Ad sales also generate reviews, which per BolKit is the larger and more lasting conversion lever; the compounding effect (rank → sales → reviews → conversion → cheaper CPC via higher expected conversion in the auction score) is the actual "flywheel" the agencies describe.

### Gaps
- No bol source quantifies rank lift per unit of ad spend or the half-life of a rank boost after ads stop; the only case study is from 2023.
- No bol-published official statement that Sponsored Products sales are weighted identically to organic sales (all statements are from agencies/tools).

## Key question 2: What ACoS/TACOS do bol sellers report in Q4, and how much do CPCs rise in November–December?

### Takeaway
bol-specific agency guidance puts "healthy" SP ACoS at 10–20% and Q4 CPC inflation at roughly 20–40% (with "2–3x in popular categories" quoted by Boloo); for a Home & Sleep product the quoted CPC band is €0.30–1.00 normally and €0.50–1.50 in peak. No bol seller or agency publishes actual Q4 ACoS/TACOS panels; the Amazon data available (Ad Badger, 2017) actually shows CPC flat and ACoS improving on BFCM because conversion rises, so CPC inflation alone does not settle whether Q4 ACoS gets worse.

### Cited Findings
- Boloo (Apr 2026) CPC table: Home & Sleep €0.30–€1.00 standard, €0.50–€1.50 peak, "medium" competition; peak periods "20-40% higher" CPC around Black Friday, Sinterklaas and Christmas; January–February lower CPCs "ideal for testing" — [Boloo, Adverteren op bol.com](https://boloo.co/blog/adverteren-op-bol-com)
- Boloo ACoS targets: aggressive growth 20–30%; profitable advertising 10–15% ("standard benchmark"); maximum profitability under 8%; healthy campaign conversion ">5%"; suggested daily minimum €10–25 to gather data; starters €150–500/month — [Boloo, Adverteren op bol.com](https://boloo.co/blog/adverteren-op-bol-com)
- Boloo (other guides, 2026): "in November and December, click prices in popular categories sometimes run 2-3x higher than normal"; overall average CPC €0.10–€0.60 (sponsored-products guide) versus €0.15–€2.50 (adverteren guide) — internal inconsistency between Boloo pages — [Boloo, Sponsored](https://boloo.co/blog/sponsored); [Boloo, Sponsored Products 2026](https://boloo.co/blog/sponsored-products)
- Boloo Q4 pacing advice: raise budget 2–3 weeks before peaks (Black Friday, Sinterklaas, Kerst); "in peak periods your daily budget runs out sooner, making you invisible at exactly the most important moments"; raise bids for top positions on high-converting products, lower on poor performers — [Boloo, Adverteren Bol.com stappenplan](https://boloo.co/blog/adverteren-bol-com)
- Bolmate (Dec 2025): target ACoS 10–20% "for healthy profit"; break-even ACoS acceptable initially for data gathering; not recommended if margin < 15% or review score < 4 stars; unnecessary if already top 3 organically; minimum CPC stated as €0.25; 14-day attribution window — [Bolmate](https://bolmate.nl/bol-sponsored-products/)
- Boloo (May 2026) states minimum CPC bid €0.04 — conflicts with Bolmate's €0.25 — [Boloo, Sponsored Products 2026](https://boloo.co/blog/sponsored-products)
- bol's own guidance: pick target ACoS from margin ("with a 20% profit margin, ACoS should stay below 20%"); allow at least 2 weeks or 80–100 clicks per product/keyword before optimizing; spend ~1.5 hours per week on campaigns; when the daily budget is spent the campaign pauses automatically, so a "generous" daily budget is advised — [bol Partnerplatform, Start je campagne SP](https://partnerplatform.bol.com/nl/idp/start-je-campagne-sp)
- OLDER, AMAZON not bol (Ad Badger, Dec 2017): Amazon SP CPC ~$0.74 baseline (17–20 Nov), unchanged on Black Friday weekend, $0.70 on Cyber Monday; conversion rose to 11.6% on Cyber Monday; ACoS 27% BF weekend, 21.9% Cyber Monday (lowest of month), 38.2% on Thanksgiving Day (worst) — [Ad Badger](https://www.adbadger.com/blog/amazon-ppc-education/amazon-black-friday-stats/)
- Haan Digital (general Dutch e-commerce CPC trends) and B&S Media give generic Dutch holiday-ads advice (CPC rises with competition; bid to stay in top 3) but no bol-specific numbers — [B&S Media](https://www.bsmedia.nl/blog/adverteren-met-sinterklaas-en-kerst-zo-knal-je-met-online-marketing-rond-de-feestdagen/)

### Inferences
- Break-even ACoS for this seller (contribution before ads / price): €16/€69,99 = 22.9%; €23/€79,99 = 28.8%. At a 15% target ACoS the allowed ad cost per sale is €10.50 (at €69,99) or €12.00 (at €79,99). At 2% click conversion that implies a break-even CPC of only €0.21 / €0.24, versus the quoted Home & Sleep CPC of €0.30–1.00 (peak €0.50–1.50). This is consistent with the AUTO campaign under-delivering: bol's algorithm will throttle bids to try to hit 15% and the daily budget will not be spent efficiently. Raising target ACoS to ~23–29% (true break-even) only makes the math work if conversion rises to ≥3.5–5%.
- Full break-even spend on 15% target: €16/day over ~45 days of Q4 ≈ €720; at €0.50 CPC that is ~1,440 clicks and ~29 orders at 2% conversion — far below what would move 248–496 pillows. Ads at current conversion are a rank/reviews lever, not a volume lever; stock will move mainly via organic/promo demand.
- Q4 direction of ACoS is unresolved for bol: agencies quote CPC inflation but the only hard data (Amazon 2017) shows conversion rising enough to lower ACoS. For a gift-appropriate home product, Sinterklaas/Christmas conversion uplift is plausible but unproven for bol.

### Gaps
- No published bol seller panel or agency benchmark for actual Q4 2024/2025 ACoS, TACOS, or measured week-by-week CPC (October → Black Friday → Sinterklaas → Christmas). The 20–40% and 2–3x figures are agency assertions without methodology.
- Nothing bol-specific on TACOS (total ad cost / total sales); only ACoS is used in bol tooling.
- Minimum CPC on bol is contradicted across sources (€0.04 vs €0.25); check in the seller's own campaign UI.

## Key question 3: Which campaign structures work on bol (AUTO vs manual, exact vs phrase, product/category vs search) and how to scale from €16/day?

### Takeaway
bol and every agency describe the same structure: AUTO for discovery, then migrate proven keywords/placements into manual campaigns with exact match and placement-specific bids, prune "bleeders" on a 2-week cycle, and keep AUTO running for residual discovery. bol's own auction (relevance × expected CTR × conversion × bid) means content/price/reviews lower CPC more than bid tuning; target ACoS in manual campaigns is reporting-only (no bid effect) as of March 2026.

### Cited Findings
- bol SP match types: exact match (keyword in quotation marks) shows only for that exact term; without quotes the keyword matches broader searches containing it ("fiets" → "blauwe fiets", "kinderfiets"); negative keywords available, and negative exact match does not spell-correct or match plurals; keywords "can now target category pages or product pages separately"; long-tail 3+ word keywords convert better — [bol Leveranciersplatform, keyword strategy](https://leveranciers.bol.com/en/need-help/advertising-via-bol/keyword-strategy/); [bol Partnerplatform, Targeting op zoekwoorden](https://partnerplatform.bol.com/nl/hulp-nodig/adverterenviabol/campagne-beheer/zoekwoorden/)
- bol advises: start with automatic campaigns; after 80–100 clicks analyze and move to manual; group related articles per campaign; add exact-match keywords for top performers and raise their bids; exclude irrelevant keywords; use the search-trends dashboard — [bol Partnerplatform, Targeting op zoekwoorden](https://partnerplatform.bol.com/nl/hulp-nodig/adverterenviabol/campagne-beheer/zoekwoorden/)
- Target ACoS is now available for manual campaigns (20 Mar 2026) but "setting a target ACoS at manual campaigns has no influence on the auction, your performance or the algorithm" — it is purely a reporting benchmark — [bol Partnerplatform, target ACoS voor handmatige campagnes](https://partnerplatform.bol.com/nl/nadp/target-acos-beschikbaar-voor-handmatige-campagnes)
- Automatic campaigns: bol selects keywords from title/description/category and sets bids toward the target ACoS; "when starting with automatic bidding you may see a higher ACoS than target due to the learning phase"; a product with many positive reviews gets lower CPC and better ad relevance — [Rylee, ACoS verlagen](https://rylee.nl/nl/blog/ACOS-sponsored-products-bol)
- Boloo notes that for maximum visibility an automatic campaign's target ACoS can be set to 50% — [Boloo, Sponsored](https://boloo.co/blog/sponsored)
- Adyard (2026) hybrid advice: automatic for the first 2–4 weeks to collect data, then manual for control per keyword, placement type and product — [Adyard, Automatisch vs handmatig](https://www.adyard.io/blog/automatisch-vs-handmatig-adverteren-op-bol-wat-werkt-beter-en-wanneer) (page later returned 404; content from search snippet)
- Bolmate 2-week action cycle: "bleeders" = 15+ clicks with zero sales or ACoS > 60% → add as exclusions; "winners" = 2+ sales and ACoS < 20% → promote to manual campaigns — [Bolmate](https://bolmate.nl/bol-sponsored-products/)
- Rylee CPC guide (Oct 2025): CPC driven by number of bidders, relevance and bid; lower CPC via content optimisation, long-tail keywords, segmenting campaigns (bestsellers / seasonal / launches), regular search-term audits, auto→manual migration at lower bids, and margin-based max CPC (example: €10 profit per unit makes a €2 CPC too high) — [Rylee, Verlaag je CPC](https://rylee.nl/nl/blog/cpc-bol-sponsored-products)
- OLDER (Nov 2023) placement comparison in one campaign: category pages had the lowest ACoS (26%), product pages 37%, search keywords 40%; advice to exclude poorly performing category pages — [MarktMentor 3/4](https://www.marktmentor.nl/blog/verbeteren-vindbaarheid-bol-sponsored-products)
- bol's "gemiddeld winnend bod" (average winning bid) per category is shown in the ad tool as a reference for manual bids — [MarktMentor, koopblok winnen](https://www.marktmentor.nl/blog/bol-sponsored-products-bieden)
- Frankwatching (Apr 2024, OLDER): new SP system launched in batches from April 2024 with improved targeting (search / category page / product page), new attribution and real-time reporting — [Frankwatching](https://www.frankwatching.com/archive/2024/04/23/sponsored-products-adverteren-via-bol/); [Follo Agency](https://folloagency.com/insights/news/new-features-bol-advertisers)
- Requirements to advertise: article must hold the buy box, complete content, correct brand, correct category — [bol Partnerplatform, Voorwaarden & tarieven SP](https://partnerplatform.bol.com/nl/idp/voorwaarden-en-tarieven-sp)

### Inferences
- With €16/day total, the seller has enough for one AUTO + one small manual campaign per country, not a full exact/phrase/product-targeting matrix. Practical scaling path from the sources: (1) pull the AUTO search-term report now (September data), (2) create a manual NL campaign with the 5–10 exact-match terms that already have ≥2 sales, bid near the category "average winning bid", (3) add negatives for bleeders in AUTO, (4) shift daily budget toward the manual campaign as it proves a lower ACoS, keep AUTO at a smaller budget for discovery, (5) raise daily budgets 2–3 weeks before Black Friday so the campaign is not paused mid-day on peak days.
- Because bol's auction score multiplies conversion into ad rank, the 2% conversion is the binding constraint on both CPC and delivery; the cheapest "ad optimisation" is listing/price/review work that raises conversion, which then lowers effective CPC in AUTO automatically.
- Category-page targeting looked cheapest in the only placement-level case; for a pillow, targeting the relevant category pages and competitor product pages in a manual campaign is worth a test, but the evidence is one 2023 example.

### Gaps
- No bol source gives a numeric bid-adjustment rule (e.g. +10% per week) or dayparting; bol has no dayparting feature documented.
- No published comparison of product-page targeting vs search on bol beyond the 2023 case.

## Key question 4: What do bol's Branded Shelves allow in 2026 (phrase match, bidding, API, reporting) and what results are reported?

### Takeaway
As of the bol help pages fetched on 2026-09-27, Branded Shelves keyword targeting is documented as exact match only, with bids settable per keyword and per category, CPC pricing, position 7 on search/category result pages, brand-registration required, and "broad match keyword targeting" and "API integration" listed as planned. Reporting via the Advertising API went live 13 Aug 2026 and its report schema includes a "keyword match type" field, which hints that more than one match type exists or is imminent, but I could not confirm a "phrase match" launch. No seller-reported ROAS/ACoS results for shelves were found; Emerce explicitly says shelves are less effective for direct conversion at low budgets.

### Cited Findings
- bol Branded Shelves targeting strategy: "your advertisement is shown when there is an exact match between the keyword set in your campaign and the keyword a customer searches for"; bids can be set per keyword and per category; category targeting can be broad (main category incl. subcategories) or specific; bidding on competitor brand keywords is allowed but competitor names may not appear in creatives; advice: "add as many relevant keywords as possible initially and optimize over time" and use keyword + category targeting together — [bol Partnerplatform, Targetingstrategie BS](https://partnerplatform.bol.com/nl/idp/targetingstrategie-bs)
- bol "Branded Shelves new developments": already live — adjust bids all at once, Brand Halo in the ad account, insights per category and keyword, improved category targeting, bids per keyword and category, no more automatic category delivery, search-term insights, campaign goals. Planned — keyword suggestions, **broad match keyword targeting**, Brand Campaign Pages, new shelf position, API integration, targeted ads, video shelf (no dates given) — [bol Partnerplatform, Branded Shelves new developments](https://partnerplatform.bol.com/en/need-help/advertising-via-bolcom/branded-shelves-new-developments/)
- Branded Shelves algorithm: relevance score = "expected number of clicks on the ad × the bid"; a relevance model means high bids alone do not win; relevance of the shelf to chosen keywords, creatives and category selection matter; the model differs from Sponsored Products so delivery patterns differ — [bol Partnerplatform, Branded Shelves algorithm](https://partnerplatform.bol.com/en/need-help/advertising-via-bolcom/the-branded-shelves-algorithm/)
- Branded Shelves reporting via Advertising API since 13 Aug 2026: "Campaign Performance Daily" (impressions served/viewable, clicks, CTR, cost, avg CPC, conversions, sales, ROAS) and "Target Placement Performance Daily" (adds ad group, target page, targeting details and **keyword match type**); combines with SP reporting in one environment — [bol developers news](https://developers.bol.com/en/news/branded-shelves-reporting-is-now-available-through-the-advertising-api/)
- Format/pricing: CPC; carousel of 3–20 products, first 3 visible without scrolling; shown at 7th position of search and category results; requires brand registration + buy box + ad approval; official launch for all partners on 2 Sep 2025 after a pilot — [MPExpert](https://mpexpert.eu/branded-shelves-explained-new-advertising-opportunity-on-bol-com/); [bol Partnerplatform, Ontdek Branded Shelves](https://partnerplatform.bol.com/nl/cdp/branded-shelves); [over.bol.com](https://over.bol.com/en/news/bol-introduceert-branded-shelves-merken-in-de-spotlight/)
- Emerce (25 Jul 2025): shelves are a mid-funnel (consideration) product; static visuals only for now; measured via a GDPR-compliant dashboard (impressions, clicks, CTR, carousel interactions); "less effective for direct conversion at lower budgets; Sponsored Products recommended instead" — [Emerce](https://www.emerce.nl/achtergrond/val-ecommerce-branded-shelves-bol)
- Rylee and 10xcrew describe shelves qualitatively (start with bestsellers on top, consistent brand identity) with no performance numbers — [Rylee, Branded Shelves](https://rylee.nl/nl/blog/branded-shelves-op-bol); [10xcrew](https://10xcrew.com/branded-shelves-now-live-on-bol-for-all-brand-owners/)
- Search snippet (source page not confirmed) mentioned a "new measuring system" for Branded Shelves from 13 January (year not stated, likely 2026) aligning definitions of clicks/impressions with Sponsored Products and warning of a trend break in results — [bol Partnerplatform news (page returned 404 on fetch)](https://partnerplatform.bol.com/en/news/new-measuring-system/)
- bol Retail Media funnel positioning: lower funnel (Sponsored Products) = "directest impact on sales, lowest ACoS"; mid-funnel (Branded Shelves) = "brand preference, average ACoS"; upper funnel = long-term awareness — [bol Retail Media, media-oplossingen](https://retailmedia.bol.com/nl/pillar/media-oplossingen/)

### Inferences
- The premise "Branded Shelves with phrase match, new in 2026" is not confirmed by bol's targeting page (exact only) and broad match is still listed as planned; the API's "keyword match type" field suggests the rollout is underway or partial. Treat any phrase/broad match availability as something to verify inside the seller's own Branded Shelves campaign UI.
- For a one-SKU private label at €2/day, a shelf mainly buys a second visible slot (position 7) on the pillow search page; bol's own funnel framing and Emerce both say it will show a higher ACoS than SP. Its useful role in Q4 is defensive brand presence on the exact term(s) the seller already ranks for, not volume.
- Since the new API reports include ROAS and match type per placement, the seller (or an n8n flow) can now pull daily shelf data and kill underperforming keywords weekly — something that was manual-only before Aug 2026.

### Gaps
- No seller- or agency-published Branded Shelves CTR, ACoS/ROAS or brand-halo numbers found anywhere (bol, Emerce, Rylee, MPExpert, 10xcrew all lack them).
- Could not retrieve the Branded Shelves Kick-off Guide PDF (404) or the Voorwaarden & tarieven page for minimum bids/daily budget.
- Could not confirm the date or content of a phrase-match launch for Branded Shelves; the "new developments" page carries no dates.

## Key question 5: What is known about bol's off-platform ads (Google Shopping/Meta) and can a seller influence them?

### Takeaway
bol funds its own Google Shopping/SEA campaigns for marketplace items and selects partner offers by price stars (≥3 stars is the eligibility threshold; ~60% of the Google Shopping budget reportedly goes to 4–5-star offers), buy-box ownership, conversion and content quality. Sellers cannot opt in, bid or pay for this; the only levers are price competitiveness, buy box, content and conversion. Paid offsite/social/display via bol Retail Media exists but is positioned for brands with managed budgets, with no self-service pricing published.

### Cited Findings
- bol: "If your article has at least 3 price stars, it becomes eligible for SEA budget" in bol's continuous Google Ads campaigns, "though there is not always budget for every category or search term"; higher price stars also give more traffic, higher search placement and more clicks/conversion; promotional campaign eligibility depends on historical and current turnover, review scores and views — [bol Partnerplatform, Prijssterren](https://partnerplatform.bol.com/nl/idp/prijssterren)
- Ecoteers: bol pays Google for Shopping placements; selection criteria are high conversion, buy-box ownership (delivery promise, competitive price, performance score), price competitiveness ("60% of bol's Google Shopping budget is spent on products with 4 or 5 price stars") and content quality (detailed descriptions, multiple images); article claims Google Shopping exposure "can increase sales by 50%" for featured products; no opt-in/out described (undated) — [Ecoteers](https://www.ecoteers.nl/blog/zo-zorg-je-ervoor-dat-bol-com-jouw-producten-kiest-voor-google-shopping/)
- bol promotions page: participating in bol promotions brings visibility "both within and outside bol.com" and partners see lasting higher search positions and more reviews — [bol Partnerplatform, Promoties via bol](https://partnerplatform.bol.com/nl/cdp/promoties-via-bol)
- bol Retail Media lists Sponsored Products, Branded Shelves, Creators for Brands, Display Advertising, Social Advertising, Demand Gen and Brand Activation Pages; no self-service/minimum budget information given — [bol Retail Media](https://retailmedia.bol.com/nl/pillar/media-oplossingen/)
- OLDER: bol's 2021 PDF "Kosteloos profiteren van Google advertenties" existed but now returns 404 — [bol Partnerplatform PDF (404)](https://partnerplatform.bol.com/content/uploads/2021/09/Kosteloos-profiteren-van-Google-advertenties.pdf)

### Inferences
- For this seller, the off-platform lever is indirect: keep ≥4 price stars at the chosen Q4 price (the star score compares against the market price level, so the €79,99 vs €69,99 decision affects eligibility), hold the buy box continuously, and lift on-bol conversion so bol's own algorithm prefers the offer for Google Shopping. bol's promotions (e.g. Black Friday/Sinterklaas deals) are the documented way to get bol to push a partner offer externally.
- There is no evidence bol offers self-service Meta/Google offsite ads to small partners in 2026; "Sponsored Display" in the Amazon sense does not exist on bol for a €16/day seller.

### Gaps
- No bol source states how much external traffic a partner product typically receives from bol's Google campaigns, or whether the "50% sales lift" claim (Ecoteers) has any basis.
- No 2025–2026 primary bol document on the SEA/Google Shopping programme beyond the price-star page; the 2021 PDF is gone.

## Key question 6: Realistic conversion rates on bol for a €70–80 home product from sponsored traffic, and how conversion changes with price stars and reviews

### Takeaway
bol-focused sources cite 5–8% conversion for well-optimised listings and ">5%" as a healthy sponsored-campaign benchmark, with a general category average around 4–5%; the seller's ~2% on ad clicks is well below those figures. Reviews (20+ at 4+ stars ≈ 2–3x sales vs 0–5 reviews) and price stars (more traffic, higher placement, higher conversion; 4 stars = "doing well") are the documented conversion drivers, but nobody publishes a price-point-specific or home-category-specific sponsored conversion rate.

### Cited Findings
- Boloo: healthy sponsored campaign conversion ">5%" — [Boloo, Adverteren op bol.com](https://boloo.co/blog/adverteren-op-bol-com)
- Search summary of Dutch sources: well-optimised bol product pages "regularly achieve 5-8% conversion"; cross-industry average 4.3% quoted as a benchmark (Opklopper / Red Factory, not bol-specific) — [Opklopper, conversie benchmarks](https://opklopper.nl/blog/conversie-webshop-verhogen); [Red Factory, verkopen op bol.com](https://redfactory.nl/kennisbank/e-commerce-webshop/verkopen-op-bolcom/)
- bol's own conversion definition: 'Mijn conversie' = my orders / my customer visits, counting only visits while the seller held the buy box — [bol Partnerplatform, Conversie analyse](https://partnerplatform.bol.com/nl/idp/conversie-analyse)
- OLDER (Nov 2023) case: 7.45% ad conversion vs "category average of ~5%" in garden/pest products; long-tail keywords converted 11–20% — [MarktMentor 3/4](https://www.marktmentor.nl/blog/verbeteren-vindbaarheid-bol-sponsored-products)
- Reviews: products with more than 10 reviews have on average 30% higher conversion than products without; 20+ reviews at 4+ stars ≈ 2–3x sales vs 0–5 reviews at equal price/buy box; aim for 20–30 reviews per product; review-request email conversion typically 3–8% (best ~14%); 6–8 weeks to statistically meaningful data; effects noticeable from 50+ orders/month — [BolKit, May 2026](https://bolkit.nl/blog/bol-com-review-conversie-benchmark-2026); [Boloo, Reviews](https://boloo.co/blog/bol-com-reviews)
- Price stars: higher stars → "more visitor traffic, higher placement in search results and more clicks", "resulting in higher conversion"; "if you have 4 price stars, you're doing well"; the score is a price level, several prices can share the same star count — [bol Partnerplatform, Prijssterren](https://partnerplatform.bol.com/nl/idp/prijssterren); [Channable, bol price stars](https://www.channable.com/blog/bol-marketplace-price-stars)
- Bolmate: do not advertise a product with review score < 4 stars or margin < 15% — [Bolmate](https://bolmate.nl/bol-sponsored-products/)
- Reviews lower CPC: "products with many positive reviews perform better in advertising campaigns, which lowers your cost per click and improves ad relevance" — [Rylee, ACoS verlagen](https://rylee.nl/nl/blog/ACOS-sponsored-products-bol)

### Inferences
- At 2% the seller converts at less than half the bol "healthy" band; fixing conversion (reviews, price stars, images/A+ style content, delivery promise) is worth more than any bid change because it simultaneously raises organic rank, lowers auction CPC and raises ad ROI. Doubling conversion to 4% would halve the break-even CPC problem noted under Q2 and make a 15–20% target ACoS attainable at €0.30–0.50 CPC.
- The €69,99 vs €79,99 choice interacts with price stars: if €79,99 drops the offer from 4 to 3 stars, the sources predict lower search placement, lower conversion and lower priority in bol's Google Shopping budget, which could outweigh the €7 extra contribution per unit. If both prices sit in the same star level (possible per bol's "price level" definition), take €79,99.
- With 248 + 248 units and ~2% conversion, the AUTO campaigns at €16/day will not sell through stock; the ads' realistic Q4 job is to seed reviews and rank in October–early November so that the organic and promo demand around Sinterklaas/Christmas lands on a better-converting listing.

### Gaps

- No published elasticity of conversion to price-star level (e.g. 3→4 stars = +x%); bol only states the direction.
- No Reddit r/bolcom or Dutch seller-forum threads with Q4 ad results surfaced in search; forum evidence is therefore absent from these notes.
